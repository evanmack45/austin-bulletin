# River beat taxonomy — jam, 2026-08-29 (late evening)

Evan asked the same five-persona panel from the selection jam to redesign
the River's beats and order. Each proposed a beat list independently and
proof-sorted the day's 39 real items. Evan chose the synthesized six-beat
map and cut the Sports beat, superseding the 2026-08-24 "beat stays"
ruling.

## Adopted (six beats; order = the Who Pays ladder)

1. **Money & bills** — any story whose news event IS a tax, fee, rate,
   bond, or budget, regardless of which body voted (Walt's collision rule).
2. **Public safety & courts** — incidents and their accountability
   stories; crashes included (a fatal wreck is a safety story).
3. **Growth & infrastructure** — routed by subject, not by which body met:
   land use, zoning, corridors, housing supply, road/transit projects,
   utilities, data centers.
4. **City Hall & county** — the governance that remains: elections,
   appointments, conduct, process.
5. **Schools** — standalone on Walt's argument: the board spends real
   money and parents are the most engaged readership.
6. **Business & street life** — venues, food, festivals, arts, community
   life as first-class economic news. Entertainment and culture live here.

**Texas is not a beat** (Mei/Walt position adopted): a statewide survivor
of the household filter sorts by its content. **Health is not a beat**:
stories route by hook. **Sports beat cut** (Evan, overturning 2026-08-24):
pro/college sports don't run; community sports may run in Business &
street life when they clear the ladder.

## Panel consensus
- All five invented a money beat at/near #1.
- All five killed Health; four of five killed Around town outright.
- Four of five moved crashes to safety; four of five cut Sports.
- Texas: three kept a thin last bucket, two cut it — Evan took the cut.

## Variants presented
- A (Mei): five beats, City Hall merged with Schools — rejected for
  burying Schools.
- B (Rosa): eight beats with standalone Roads & transit and a visible
  thin Texas bucket.
- C (Walt): seven beats with a "Power, Water & Health" systems beat — he
  flagged its hospital-accountability hole himself.
- Adopted: Mei's spine + Walt's Schools repair = six.
