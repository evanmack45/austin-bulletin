# What makes a daily local-news product worth returning to — research notes

Date: 2026-08-23. Method: web research by a subagent (10-call budget), sources
from 2024–2026. Feeds `docs/2026-08-23-site-plan.md`.

## Examples studied

| Name | Format | Daily sections in order | Length | Utility modules | Image approach | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Axios Local — Axios Austin | Daily M–F email, 6:30am | News → Things to Do → Food and Drink → Politics → Real Estate → Business → Sports → Wellness Brief | 3–4 min; 5–6 items in Smart Brevity | Things to Do calendar, Real Estate brief, Wellness Brief, Sports | Sparse; emoji per section; one lead image | Terse, scannable, bolded why-it-matters lines; one local writer's voice |
| 6AM City — ATXtoday | Daily M–F email | Weather with sunrise/sunset → Lead story → Engagement module (poll/RSVP) → Events & News roundup → Personal editor wrap-up | 750–1,000 words, ~5 min | Weather w/ sunrise-sunset, polls, RSVP blocks, curated events, "Wrap" section | Emoji per section; colored borders on engagement modules; photo in the Wrap | Upbeat, positive-only; avoids politics/crime; community pride |
| Naptown Scoop (Annapolis, indie) | Daily indie newsletter | "What's Happenin'" events calendar + referral-program mention in template | Short per item | Events calendar is the core utility; referral milestones | Minimal | Personal, hyper-local, written to one imagined reader |
| From Boise / Wichita Life / Catskill Crew / Ottawa Lookout (indie, beehiiv case studies) | Twice-weekly to daily | Curated events/culture + membership extras + monthly plan-ahead guide | Varies; cadence consistency valued over length | Monthly events mega-guide, paid perks, polls | Instagram-first teasers | Founder-as-local-character; unchanging format treated as a loyalty asset |
| Austin Monitor → Austin Current | Weekday morning "Daily Headlines"; nonprofit relaunch 2025→2026 | Headline digest of civic/government stories ("What We Woke Up To") | Not stated; anti-clickbait | Weekly recap | Not detailed | Sober civic-accountability register |
| KUT daily newsletter | Weekday evening (relaunched 2024) | Local reporting + curated stories from other outlets + reporter insight | Not stated | Things-to-do, cross-outlet curation | Not detailed | Named "real human," first person, reply-based reader Q&A |

## Patterns that drive daily return visits

1. A fixed, unchanging structural template (6AM City; From Boise never changed its cadence).
2. Genuine daily utility, not just news — 6AM City opens every issue with sunrise/sunset-anchored weather.
3. A named, personal author who replies to readers (KUT; Inbox Collective).
4. Referral programs work only after critical mass and with a cheap, screenshot-worthy first milestone (Naptown Scoop: 98.7% never pass the first milestone).
5. Loyalty over reach is the 2025–2026 consensus (Nieman Lab: "Loyalty, not scale, is key"; "What problem are we solving, and for whom?").
6. Local hard news converts to paid loyalty better than lifestyle content (Sebastopol Times 24.5% paid conversion vs ~3% for national Substacks).
7. A closing personal/editor note builds felt connection (Mirror Indy, 608% first-year growth).
8. Monthly forward-looking guides serve retention and acquisition at once (From Boise).

## Patterns that fail or annoy readers

1. Over-formatted, hard-to-scan text (6AM City stripped bolding/italics/links after complaints).
2. Changing a working engagement mechanic breaks it (Naptown Scoop referral milestone swap "completely bombed").
3. Unmoderated comment sections become a time sink or turn hostile.
4. Promotion that is pure "sign up" asks reads as spam; every promo post must be useful on its own.
5. Politics/crime is a churn risk in lifestyle newsletters (6AM City avoids them) — a tone trade-off, not a universal rule; civic outlets like Austin Current lean the other way.

## Ideas specific to Austin (data a daily could own)

1. Highland Lakes / Lake Travis levels — LCRA daily reports; Water Data for Texas per-reservoir percent-full (daily/weekly/monthly comparisons).
2. Barton Springs water temperature — USGS gauge 08155500 (live temperature, discharge, historical daily stats).
3. ERCOT grid demand — public System-Wide Demand dashboard (actual vs forecast).
4. Air quality / ozone — TCEQ Austin-region monitors (ozone, PM2.5, wind, temperature).
5. City Council agenda tracker — austincouncil.app already does previews and plain-English recaps; a Bulletin-owned version fills the civic-calendar slot.
6. A "what we woke up to" recap module — the civic audience already expects a distilled anti-clickbait morning digest (Austin Current).
