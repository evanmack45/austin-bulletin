# Regulars Review: The Austin Bulletin — 2026-08-23

Smoke test of the live site (https://theaustinbulletin.com) by six role-played
readers, each with one task and one unhappy path. Run via
`/review-squad:regulars`, sequentially, in the publisher's Chrome. Readers did
not read source code. Each reader had a 10-tool-call budget.

## Scorecard

| # | Task | Persona | Result | Issues |

## What Worked Well

- Page loads fast; every reader rated time-to-complete "fast".
- Broadsheet masthead fits at 375 px wide with no sideways overflow; body text is readable one-thumbed.
- Archive and About links are in the header on every page; every reader found them without hunting.
- AI disclosure and corrections policy on the About page are plain and clearly worded.
- Tab titles carry the site name and the edition date.
- Old URL (`evanmack45.github.io/austin-bulletin/`) and `www` both redirect to `https://theaustinbulletin.com/`.
- `/archive` works with and without the trailing slash.
- Four of six readers said they would come back; the two who would not (Priya, Sam) each named one missing thing (a real contact channel; a feed).

## Not verified

- Devon (reader 2) ran out of budget after pressing Back on the source article; whether Back returned him to his scroll position is unconfirmed.
