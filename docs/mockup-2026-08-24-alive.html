The Big Story

Pavement or rail? Central Texas is betting on both

TxDOT is widening Interstate 35 between Round Rock and Georgetown, a stretch that recorded 4,114 crashes on its main lanes and 31 deaths from 2021 through 2026, according to the agency's own count. The project adds lanes and rebuilds interchanges along one of the busiest corridors in Central Texas. A few miles away, a different kind of capacity question is on the table: a feasibility study commissioned by Travis County found no engineering dealbreakers for a 125 mph passenger rail line between Austin and San Antonio, running largely along the SH 130 and I-10 corridors, at an estimated cost of roughly $13.5 billion.

The road and the possible railway won't compete for the same trip for years, if ever — the environmental review for I-35 alone runs past the decade's end. Meanwhile, CapMetro is pausing Red Line service Aug. 24–27 for track work at the future North Burnet/Uptown station, a smaller but more immediate reminder that both driving and transit in the region are under construction at once.

What's next: TxDOT's environmental review continues; completion is projected for late 2029. The rail study goes to Travis County commissioners next.
