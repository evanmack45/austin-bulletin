## Things worth the publisher's eye

- **`npm run video` is down, so the bulletin has no video today.** YouTube's oEmbed
  endpoint returns HTTP 401 for every request from this machine, not just for one
  clip: `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=…` returns "Unauthorized" for a FOX 7
  clip on repeated tries, with and without our User-Agent. The YouTube *channel*
  feeds (`/feeds/videos.xml`) work fine, so this is oEmbed specifically. The
  2026-08-25 log recorded a single 401 on one clip and read it as per-video; a day
  later it is every clip, which reads more like YouTube blocking datacenter IPs on
  that endpoint. I confirmed a third-party oEmbed mirror answers 200, and did **not**
  use it — `scripts/video.mjs` is the documented path and routing around a blocked
  endpoint through someone else's proxy is not a call an unattended run should make.
  Two good clips went unused: FOX 7's "Federal prosecutors pursue dozens of
  gun-related crimes in Austin" and "Central Texas fire chiefs speak on wildfire
  mitigation," both matching River items. If this persists, the fix is a decision
  for you: either accept video-free bulletins on days YouTube blocks us, or point
  `video.mjs` at a different metadata route.
- **Sources disagree on the Flock numbers, and the Big Story says so.** CBS Austin
  reports nearly 380 unauthorized agencies with access and quotes Coffman on 80
  approved vs. 450+ found; FOX 7 quotes Coffman on 459 outside organizations and
  nearly 1.6 million searches against 78 listed on Flock's site. Camera counts
  differ too (28 vs. 32). Rather than pick one, the story attributes each figure to
  the outlet that published it and states outright that the reported figures differ,
  per accuracy rule 4.
- **The Pflugerville council outcome is reported, not observed.** CBS Austin's
  headline says the council denied the new agreement and its body says leaders
  "indicated they are likely to cut ties"; FOX 7 says the city plans to move on and

  this is a rendering problem rather than a policy one. The Big Story therefore says
  the council "did not approve a new contract, CBS Austin reported" and names no vote
  tally. If a tally matters, it will be in the minutes later this week.
