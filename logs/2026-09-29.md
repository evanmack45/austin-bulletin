## Facts I was unsure about

  CBS Austin calls him a Mississippi man and places the apartment in Wells Branch rather
  than "north Travis County." The item states neither residence and uses KXAN's
  geography, since KXAN is working from the arrest affidavit. If a second outlet pins
  the location more precisely, tomorrow's item should say so.

  months" and "in a few weeks." The brief drops the timing entirely and carries the ICE
  spokesperson's statement instead.

  passed into law under Texas Senate Bill 3, Travis County has plans to install flood
  warning sirens by August 2027." That is the county's plan under SB 3, not necessarily
  a date the statute names, and the item is worded that way.
