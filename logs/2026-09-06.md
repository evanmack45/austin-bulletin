## Site defect found during the live check (not fixed — for Evan)

Verifying the published page turned up a bug outside today's edition: **the
Atom feed at /feed.xml is serving the ten *oldest* bulletins, not the ten
newest.** It currently ends at Sept. 1, so anyone subscribed has not seen a new
edition in five days.

Cause: `eleventy.config.js` sorts the `bulletins` collection newest-first
(line 90), but `feedPlugin` re-orders entries by date ascending before applying
`collection.limit: 10`, so the limit keeps the earliest ten. The standing
decision of 2026-08-23 specifies the ten newest.

Not changed in this run — site changes beyond the daily bulletin are Evan's
call (CLAUDE.md working agreements), and this does not block the edition. The
fix is small: sort the collection oldest-first for the feed, or drop
`collection.limit` and slice the newest ten into a dedicated collection for the
plugin. Say the word and it goes in.
