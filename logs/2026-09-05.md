## Gate

`npm run build` clean. `npm run check -- 2026-09-05` failed on the first pass
with six problems — three briefs over the 35-word cap, "ISD" used ahead of its
expansion (the hero chart's alt text and caption both said "Austin ISD" and
they sit above the prose that expands it), and both graphics reading as empty
alt text. The last was self-inflicted: `scripts/graphic.mjs` prints its alt
attribute with `&apos;` for apostrophes and the checker's attribute regex stops
at a raw `'`, so the hand-pasted version matched nothing. Fixed by re-rendering
both specs and pasting the script's output verbatim, and by rewriting the
chart's title, subtitle, caption and alt to spell the district out. Adding AAA
to `scripts/acronyms.json` converted its warning into a hard failure, which is
the intended behavior — expanded to "American Automobile Association" in the
copy. Second and third passes clean, all 25 links verified.

Read top to bottom afterward, which caught six things the checker cannot see: a
lead headline whose figure was not in its own body, a brief opening "The
department also" and leaning on the previous item's subject, "the November
cuts" doing the same to the Briefing, a season opener named without saying
whose, and two broken closing sentences in The Number and One Good Thing.
