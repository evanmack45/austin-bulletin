## The day's idea

The Briefing leads on Austin ISD's air conditioning. Kealing Middle School's
chiller failed again Sept. 22, making it the fourth campus since late August
to write families that the building would not cool, after McCallum, Anderson
and Lamar Middle School — two of which sent students home early. The thread
that made it the day's idea rather than a single story: the same district is
weeks from a state intervention decision, Kealing is one of the nine campuses
that inherited four consecutive failing ratings when the Texas Education
Agency reassigned six closed schools' records (our own 09-23 chart), and the
district finished bond-funded HVAC work at 15 campuses over the summer. All
of it lands in the hottest September on record to date.

Not the ICE shooting, for the third morning running. It is still the largest
continuing local story and it carries seven items and the video in Public
safety & courts, but it led the Briefing on 09-22 and the topic-concentration
check in EDITORIAL's neutrality rules exists precisely to stop a subject from
leading by default. Yesterday's Briefing was the DPS registration failure;
that story also stayed in the City Desk today rather than repeating as a
Briefing, with its own chart.
