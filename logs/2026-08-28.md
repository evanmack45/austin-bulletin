## Big Story selection, and the data-center concentration check

The day's obvious candidate was the City Council's unanimous vote to
fast-track data center rules — five outlets had it, and Gov. Abbott's
statewide "pause" and a Texas Tribune piece on data centers versus farmland
would have connected to it cleanly.

It was not chosen as the lead. Data centers led the Big Story on 2026-08-24
("Austin starts writing rules for the data centers it does not have yet") and
came close again on 08-25. EDITORIAL's topic-concentration check exists so
that a repeat is a decision rather than a default; the decision here is that
Thursday's storm was both fresher and more consequential to daily life — a
worker was killed — and that a fourth data-center-heavy morning inside a week
would make the paper read as being about one thing. The council vote runs as
the lead River item in City Hall & county with a follow-up item and the FOX 7
video, and Abbott's pause and the farmland story run in Texas. Nothing was
lost but the top slot.

## Facts I was unsure about

- **KXAN's Lake Travis water story contradicts itself.** It reports that about
  5.27 million gallons went out in "irrigation-sized bursts" between 7 p.m.
  Wednesday and 6 a.m. Thursday, and then that 88 percent of that
  irrigation-sized use — "roughly 10.4 million gallons" — fell outside
  permitted Stage 3 hours. 88 percent of 5.27 million is about 4.6 million, so
  the two figures cannot both be right. The bulletin uses only the 5.27
  million figure and the utility's production-versus-use pair (about 14
  million produced, about 14.8 million used). The 88 percent / 10.4 million
  claim was left out rather than picked between.
- **Boil water notice, or pressure?** CBS Austin says the three Lake Travis
  ISD campuses were affected by "a boil water notice, which turned into low
  water pressure issues"; KVUE's headline says a boil notice remains for some
  West Travis County PUA customers; KXAN describes low or no water pressure
  and does not mention a boil notice at the schools. The Big Story reports the
  pressure loss and the bottled water and hand sanitizer, both of which every
  source agrees on, and does not assert a boil-water notice at the campuses.
- **Ross Fire figures moved.** The Texas Tribune had 85,000 acres and 21
  percent containment as of Thursday afternoon; yesterday's edition carried
  KXAN at nearly 82,000 acres and 12 percent on Wednesday. Used the Tribune's
  Thursday numbers and said "since Monday" rather than reconciling the two.
- **Meta settlement totals still disagree across outlets.** A CBS Austin video
  headline says $18 billion where FOX 7 said nearly $17 billion on Wednesday.
  No Meta item ran today, so nothing turned on it, but the discrepancy is
  worth knowing about before the next one.

  unnamed people, relayed by the Austin Business Journal through KXAN. Run
