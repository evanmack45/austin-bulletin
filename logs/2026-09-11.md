## Facts I was careful about

- **No ages calculated.** The police releases give dates of birth for John
  Michael Filley and Kim Bao Hoang but no ages, and deriving an age from a
  birth date is a calculation, so neither man's age is printed. Lilliana
  Segura's age is printed because CBS Austin and the police release both
  state it.
- **The fatality chart compares like with like.** It uses the police
  department's own same-date comparison (57 deaths this year against 75 by
  this date last year), not the full-year totals KXAN also publishes, so a
  partial year is never set beside a complete one. The KXAN annual figures
  were left out of the chart for that reason.
- **Redfin's ranking was narrowed.** KXAN reports Austin's 82.3% as the
  highest of the three Texas metros Redfin listed, not of every Texas
  metro; the item says so.
- **The Lake Austin figures are attributed**, not asserted: 200 jobs and
  $2.5 million over six weeks is Ken Leonard's estimate to Council.
- **Both sides in their own words** on the two contested items — the
  density bonus vote (Awais Azhar and Greg Anderson) and the mail-ballot
  suit (County Clerk Dyana Limon-Mercado and the executive order's own
