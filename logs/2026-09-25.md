## Code changes this run

Two defects logged on 2026-09-23 and left unfixed are fixed:

1. `scripts/card.mjs` cut a post body at exactly 400 characters with no
   ellipsis, which published a card ending mid-word. It now cuts at a
   sentence end inside the budget where one exists, otherwise at a word
   break, and marks the cut with an ellipsis. Two of today's cards needed it.
2. `scripts/check.mjs` read image attributes with a `(["'])([^"']*)\1`
   pattern, which cannot express "anything but the delimiter" — so a
   double-quoted `alt` holding a raw apostrophe did not match at all and the
   gate reported the image as having no alt text. Today's hero chart tripped
   it. Now one pattern per quote style.

Also added `AED` to `scripts/acronyms.json` and `KTBC` to the call-sign
ignore list in `scripts/acronyms.mjs`.
