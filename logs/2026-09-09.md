## The day's idea

Central Health's board sets its fiscal 2027 tax rate today, choosing between
a 6%–8% increase and a no-new-revenue rate. Household money, dated and
quantified — top of the Who Pays ladder — and the decision the 2026-09-05
Briefing's "What's next" line pointed readers toward. The Briefing carries
both sides from FOX 7's own reporting (Dr. Patrick Lee for the district;
Lago Vista Mayor Shane Saum and Matt Mackowiak of Save Austin Now against),
plus the ADP wage figure as the other thing that moved on the household
ledger this year.

Considered and set aside as the Briefing subject, with reasons:

- **Austin ISD's money.** Strong, but the "nonprofits fill the gap" angle was
  the 2026-09-06 Schools lead, and today's Austin Current story largely
  restates it. Only the genuinely new fact — that the $205M package cut $1
  million for Communities in Schools — ran, as a brief. The third campus to
  lose cooling in under two weeks became the Schools lead instead.
- **DPS.** Tempting to thread the Cisco's arrests (DPS called ICE after a
  traffic stop) to DPS's 940-camera network, but no source connects them.
  That would have been a manufactured connection, so both ran as separate
  leads in Public safety & courts.
- **Red River.** Genuinely new (the board's open letter, and a musician
  starting a nonprofit), but the paper has run Red River venue losses on
  08-29, 08-30, 09-02 and 09-07. It led Business & street life instead.
- Topic concentration: cameras led the Briefing on 09-07 and Central Health
  on 09-03. Central Health leading again is a decision, not a default — today
  is the vote itself, not another proposal story.
