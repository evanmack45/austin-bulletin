## Corrections to my own work during the run

The school-tax bar chart first rendered on an auto-scaled axis starting near
$850, which turned a 13.6% drop into what looked like a collapse. Re-rendered
with `yMin: 0`. Worth recording: `scripts/graphic.mjs` auto-scales by default,
and for a small percentage change on a large base that is actively misleading.
Any future two-bar chart of a modest change should set `yMin` explicitly.

## Site fix shipped alongside the edition

While verifying the published edition I checked `/feed.xml` and found it
serving the **ten oldest** bulletins (Aug. 23 – Sept. 1), not the ten newest
that the standing decision specifies. It had been wrong since the feed
shipped, which means no RSS subscriber has ever received a new edition.

Cause: `@11ty/eleventy-plugin-rss` renders `collection | reverse | head(limit)`,
which assumes Eleventy's default oldest-first collection order. Our `bulletins`
collection is deliberately sorted newest-first for the homepage and archive, so
the plugin's reverse handed it the oldest end of the archive.

Fix: a separate ascending `bulletinsFeed` collection for the plugin to consume.
`bulletins` is untouched, so the homepage and archive keep their order. Pushed
as its own commit and verified live: the feed now leads with today's edition.
