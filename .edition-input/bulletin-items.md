# The Austin Bulletin — Fri Nov. 13, 2026 (draft, Marrow)

## News

**Bulletin original: Austin's new-house permits rise a third straight year**
The City issued 1,845 permits for new single-family houses from January through October, up 5.9% from the same months of 2025 and 36.9% from 2023.
Why it matters: more houses are entering the pipeline, though a permit isn't a finished home. Duplexes and apartments aren't counted.
Source: [City of Austin, Issued Construction Permits (3syk-w9eu)](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/3syk-w9eu), queried Nov. 13

**Convention Center picks 10 local artists for $17.7 million in built-in art**
The works will be part of the building itself, inside and along outdoor plazas, in the $1.6 billion redevelopment. A second round of smaller commissions is expected in 2027.
Source: [City of Austin release, Nov. 12](https://www.austintexas.gov/arts-culture/news/austin-convention-center-announces-10-local-artists-bring-creative-spirit)

**South Austin burglary victims: police may have your stolen property**
After an arrest tied to eight burglaries near the Williamson Creek Greenbelt, APD recovered jewelry, coins, passports and firearms. Some items haven't been reported stolen yet. Report at 3-1-1 or iReportAustin.com.
Source: [Austin Police release, Nov. 12](https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/apd-detectives-arrest-suspect-connection-series-burglaries)

**Police seek video after 12 tire slashings in southeast Austin**
Most happened overnight on weekdays at apartment complexes around the Mabel Davis area since August. Residents with cameras can upload footage through APD's evidence portal.
Source: [Austin Police release, Nov. 10](https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/apd-investigating-multiple-tire-slashing-incidents-southeast-austin)
