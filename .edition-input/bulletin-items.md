# The Austin Bulletin — Sat Nov. 14, 2026 (draft, Marrow)

## News

**Bulletin original: Catalytic converter theft reports surge back in Austin**
Austin police logged 297 catalytic converter thefts from January through October, up from 32 in the same months of 2025 and 29 in 2024. That's still far below the 2022 peak of 1,763. Most of this year's reports (161) were at parking lots or garages.
Why it matters: a crime that had nearly vanished is back. APD says southeast Austin apartment lots were hit 31 times since August and suggests shields, cameras and VIN etching.
Source: [City of Austin, Crime Reports (fdj4-gpfu)](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/fdj4-gpfu), queried Nov. 14; [Austin Police release, Nov. 13](https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/austin-police-arrest-man-found-six-catalytic-converters)

**Council hears plan for emergency demolitions of dangerous buildings Nov. 19**
The change would let the City's building official quickly tear down structures that pose an imminent danger, without waiting for a Building and Standards Commission order. Thursday's item is a public hearing only; no vote is posted. The commission backed the idea 6-1 in September.
Source: [Nov. 19 agenda backup, item 85](https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=485440)

**Council weighs $58.5 million for Hornsby Bend plant upgrades**
Two construction contracts ($22.4 million for headworks improvements, $32.9 million for an ammonia-removal facility and drainage) plus a $3.2 million engineering amendment for Austin Water's biosolids plant are on the Nov. 19 agenda. A water commission recommended both contracts 6-0.
Source: [Nov. 19 final agenda, items 14, 15 and 94](https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=485293)

**MoPac crash is Austin's 80th deadly crash this year**
A driver died after a two-vehicle collision Wednesday late morning in the 8100 block of northbound MoPac; two people in the other vehicle had non-life-threatening injuries. Austin has 82 traffic deaths this year, compared with 86 at this point in 2025.
Source: [Austin Police release, Nov. 13](https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/traffic-fatality-8100-block-north-mopac-expressway-nb)

**Police seek man in downtown parking-garage sexual assault**
APD says a man assaulted a victim around 1:11 a.m. Nov. 1 in the garage at 405 Colorado St. and struck the victim when pushed away. He was slim and wore a white long-sleeved shirt, black hat, vest and pants, and a shoulder holster. Call detectives at 512-974-8512.
