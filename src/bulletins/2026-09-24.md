### Four campuses lost their cooling in a month, in the hottest September on record

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-24/aisd-hvac-failures.png" alt="Timeline of air conditioning failures reported at Austin Independent School District campuses during the 2026-27 school year. <figcaption>Timeline: The Austin Bulletin · Austin ISD principals&apos; letters to families and district statements, via KXAN</figcaption>

Kealing Middle School's chiller quit again on Sept. 22. It was the fourth Austin Independent School District (ISD)
campus since late August to write home and say the building would not cool, after McCallum, Anderson and Lamar Middle
School. Two of them sent students home early. At Anderson the principal said the system was cooling 40% of
the building and classrooms had reached the mid-80s.

The heat did not help. KXAN's forecast team called this the hottest September on record to date, after
the fifth-hottest August, and the National Weather Service put today's heat index at 106.

The district has been spending on this. Austin ISD finished bond-funded air conditioning work at 15 campuses over the
summer and opened four fully modernized schools in August.

Kealing is on a second list too. The Texas Education Agency (TEA) moved six closed schools' rating histories onto the
campuses that took their students, and Kealing became one of nine Austin ISD campuses carrying four consecutive failing
years. Two district middle schools drew a fifth failing rating this year. Under state law that requires the education
commissioner to close them or replace the elected board.

#### Money & bills

##### Austin Community College set its tax rate, and the average bill drops about five dollars
Trustees approved $0.103643 per $100 of valuation on Sept. 14, a hair above last year's $0.1034. Because taxable values
fell further than the rate rose, the college said the owner of an average home will pay about $557 this year, down from
$562. Officials tied the increase to debt on the $770 million bond voters approved in
2022. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/education/austin-community-college-adopts-tax-rate-0-24-increase-from-last-year/">Community Impact</a></span>

##### City staff read 133 social service contracts and recommended cutting none of them
The city's budget director told a council work session Tuesday that the office reviewed $48.2 million across eight
departments and proposed keeping 106 agreements and modifying 27. The budget year opened with $16.8 million in proposed
reductions before council restored $4.7 million. Twenty-six of the evaluations flagged compliance
concerns. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/no-funding-cuts-recommended-after-city-staff-review-133-social-service-agreements/">KXAN</a></span>

The city's whole social services portfolio runs about $101.4 million this budget year, with the largest shares going to homelessness services and public health. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/austin-s-social-services-spending-reset-continues/">Community Impact</a></span>

Property taxes fund about 67% of Austin Community College's $583 million budget, and that revenue fell this year for the first time in 17 years. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/education/austin-community-college-adopts-tax-rate-0-24-increase-from-last-year/">Community Impact</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### Almost every Travis County arrestee now has a lawyer standing beside them at the bail hearing
The American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU) of Texas said this month that 99% of eligible arrestees have had an attorney
at their first court appearance since January, seven months into the settlement it and VOCAL-TX reached with the county
in December 2025. The gap is the overnight shift: 20 people from April through July went without counsel because of the
hour. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austinchronicle.com/news/after-aclu-settlement-99-of-travis-county-arrestees-get-a-lawyer-at-bail-hearings/">Austin Chronicle</a></span>
