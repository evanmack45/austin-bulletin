## The Briefing

### Austin has never had a six weeks this hot

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-13/hundred-degree-days.png" alt="Bar chart of the number of days reaching 100 degrees or hotter at Camp Mabry in Austin between August 1 and September 12, for the four highest years on record: 39 days in 2026, 38 days in 2023, 38 days in 2019 and 36 days in 2011">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · KXAN First Warning Weather, from Camp Mabry records</figcaption>
</figure>

From Aug. 1 through Saturday, Camp Mabry recorded 39 days of 100 degrees or hotter out of 43. No year on record has
done that over the same six weeks. The closest are 2023 and 2019, at 38 days each, and the summer of 2011, at 36. The
coolest high in the entire run was 94 degrees, and that was Aug. 1 itself. KXAN's meteorologists put the streak down to
a high-pressure ridge that is also keeping the rain away, and they expect it to hold through the end of next week.

Saturday showed the cost. The University of Texas at Austin expected more than 100,000 people at Darrell K Royal-Texas
Memorial Stadium (DKR) and set out 19 free water stations for them. Austin-Travis County Emergency Medical Services
(ATCEMS) added units, a week after it carried 13 people to hospitals from the season's first game, at least three of
them with heat illness. Off East Dean Keeton Street, a graduate student's heat stress tracker read between 119 and 121
degrees in the sun.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> A Heat Advisory runs until 7 p.m. today, and the National Weather Service forecasts 101 again on Monday and Tuesday.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### The city adds $200,000 to the contract of a nonprofit that ran out of money
City Council approved a one-time $200,000 addition on Thursday to SAFE Alliance's contract to run the city-owned
domestic violence shelter, bringing the agreement to $12.4 million. The nonprofit warned in April that it was running
short, and closed Eloise House, its Southeast Austin clinic, on June 8. That clinic had performed most of the
forensic exams for sexual assault survivors in Travis County. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/local/article/austin-affordable-housing-project-connect-22427237.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

Travis County now has one community-based forensic exam center. BRAVE Alliance handles most of that work under an agreement with Ascension Seton, Baylor Scott & White Health and St. David's HealthCare. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/local/article/austin-affordable-housing-project-connect-22427237.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### A three-and-a-half-week police push against drunk driving ends with eight arrests
Austin police ran a driving while intoxicated (DWI) enforcement and no-refusal initiative between Aug. 14 and Sept. 7.
Officers made eight arrests, issued 83 citations and gave 132 warnings. The department said the no-refusal part of it
meant applying for blood search warrants when a driver arrested for DWI declined to give a breath or blood sample.

#### City Hall & county

##### East Austin's Millennium moves under city governance
Council voted Thursday to draft an ordinance creating a city advisory board for the Millennium Youth Entertainment
Complex, the East Austin bowling and skating center run since 1995 by the Rosewood Community Development Corporation.
The corporation handed day-to-day management to city staff in October 2025 and its own board asked for the change in
March. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/local/article/austin-affordable-housing-project-connect-22427237.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

Dewi Smith, the corporation's vice chair, told Council she hoped the center's mission and history would not be forgotten. Council Member Natasha Harper-Madison thanked the volunteers who had kept it running. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/local/article/austin-affordable-housing-project-connect-22427237.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>
