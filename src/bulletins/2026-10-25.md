## The Briefing

{% bigstory %}
### A quarter of Travis County voters have already cast ballots.

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-25/early-ballots.png" alt="Bar chart from Travis County unofficial early voting totals through Oct. 24: 237,489 ballots cast and 948,575 registered voters.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County</figcaption>
</figure>

Through Saturday, 237,489 people had voted early in person or by mail, 25.04% of the county's 948,575 registered voters, according to the county's unofficial daily totals. In-person turnout ran between about 37,800 and 40,800 a day on weekdays, then dropped to 24,575 on Saturday.

Early voting continues.

Early voting continues through Friday, Oct. 30, at any Travis County polling site, and the county says some sites will have extended hours on the last two days. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. Check today's hours and locations before you go.

The City's festival of trees, pollinators and urban nature runs through Nov. 1, with more than 100 free events across the city, including tree plantings, guided nature walks and pollinator programs. The full schedule, searchable by date, neighborhood and activity, is at rootsandwingsfest.com.

Austin Economic Development's nine-question survey takes 10 to 15 minutes, is in English and Spanish, and stays open until community listening sessions wrap up in early 2027. It asks about Austin's economic challenges, City help for businesses, and which community benefits to prioritize as the city grows.

A virtual open house on the first amendment to the Travis County Transportation Blueprint, the 30-year transportation plan for unincorporated parts of the county that Commissioners Court approved in 2019, is open Oct. 19 through Nov. 20.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Early voting continues through Friday, Oct. 30, at any Travis County polling site.</p>
