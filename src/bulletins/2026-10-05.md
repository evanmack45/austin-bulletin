### Today is the last day to register for November

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-05/registration-deadline.png" alt="Deadline-day submission guidance for Monday, October 5, 2026: take a completed application to a Travis County tax office; the Nelda Wells Spears Building at 2433 Ridgepoint Drive has special hours from 8 a.m. to midnight today only; or mail an application postmarked October 5 or earlier. The county recommends requesting a manual postmark at the postal counter.">
<figcaption>Graphic: The Austin Bulletin &middot; Travis County Tax Office</figcaption>
</figure>

Today, Oct. 5, is the registration deadline for the Nov. 3 election. For a new resident, a student registering for the first time or someone whose name or address changed, the useful first step is checking the official registration instructions and your current record.

The ordinary Secretary of State new-registration web form is a way to prepare an application: fill it out, print it, sign it and mail or deliver it to your county voter registrar. Completing that form online alone does not finish registration. Existing Texas voters can update their records online; separate instructions cover registration through qualifying Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) license or identification-card transactions.

Travis County offers a deadline-day option at the Nelda Wells Spears Building, 2433 Ridgepoint Drive: registration hours there run from 8 a.m. to midnight today only. The extended hours apply to that building, not every county office.

If you mail an application, it must be postmarked Oct. 5 or earlier. The county warns that putting it in a postal collection box can produce a late postmark. It recommends asking for a manual postmark at the postal counter, or delivering the completed application to a tax office instead.

##### Municipal Court's new docket schedule starts today
Austin Municipal Court's schedule change takes effect today: attorney appearance dockets and jury trials will be held on Tuesdays. The court says the consolidation follows a review of underused docket times. Its walk-in mitigation schedule stays unchanged, so people seeking to discuss cases with a judge or prosecutor still have separate morning, afternoon and Thursday evening options. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/austin-municipal-court-streamlines-docket-schedule">City of Austin</a></span>

Walk-in mitigation at 6800 Burleson Road runs Monday–Thursday, 8:30–11 a.m. and 1:30–3:30 p.m., plus Thursday, 6–8 p.m.; call 512-974-4800 with court questions. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/austin-municipal-court-streamlines-docket-schedule">City of Austin</a></span>
