### Hundreds of homes cleared paperwork this week. None has broken ground.

<figure class="graphic">

Austin City Council gave final approval Thursday to rezoning the old Rosedale School at 2117 W. 49th St., which clears
the way for a 435-unit, six-story building after three postponements since May. The vote was the easy part. Austin
Independent School District (AISD) agreed last year to sell the closed campus for $26 million, money it needs against a
shortfall that had it planning to borrow $140 million in October to make payroll, and the sale still turns on the
district beating a neighborhood lawsuit over a 1938 deed restriction. No trial date is set.

"I'm disappointed that even though today we're going to approve zoning for the former Rosedale School, that will not
lead to housing being built," District 7 Council Member Mike Siegel said. Rosedale resident Daniel Ray, a defendant in
the district's suit, told council that neighbors are prepared to carry the fight to the next stage.

The St. John site had a different obstacle: money, not paper. The land at 800 E. St. John Ave. has been city-owned
since 2013 and empty for close to two decades, with 201 affordable units stalled about five years behind a funding
gap. Airbnb said Friday it is putting $6.4 million in to close that gap.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The Rosedale sale waits on Travis County District Court, where the district's suit over the 1938 deed restriction has no trial date. The rest of the week's city money is in <a href="#money-bills">Money &amp; bills</a>, and the rest of its building decisions in <a href="#growth-infrastructure">Growth &amp; infrastructure</a>.</p>

##### Council put $2.18 million behind small business lending
Three contracts advanced Thursday: up to $1.5 million for a loan interest buydown pilot run by the Austin nonprofit
PeopleFund, $432,000 for the San Francisco nonprofit Kiva to match crowdfunded microloans, and $250,000 for a pitch
competition with the McCombs School of Business at the University of Texas at Austin. Economic Development Director Mac
Cummins said access to capital is still the biggest obstacle local businesses
face. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/austin-expands-small-business-support-with-2-18m-for-new-programs/">Community Impact</a></span>

##### A downtown high-rise has been dark for nine days, and the tenants want rent credits
The Bowie at 311 Bowie St. has been without power since an electrical fire in a hallway utility room on Sept. 17, which
the Austin Fire Department said Friday was caused by overheated wires. Power is estimated back Sept. 29. The Bowie
Tenants Union, which is not affiliated with management, said residents are being paid for hotels and meals in Travis
County but are getting no rent credits. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/local/power-outages-downtown-austin-bowie-apartments/269-a7cef0f6-78d4-4fe5-818e-16e2a17219a3">KVUE</a></span>

Council renewed two contracts with Catholic Charities of Central Texas for immigration legal services and filing fee assistance, together about $2.6 million, an increase of roughly $300,000 over last year. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/austin-funded-immigration-programs-meet-citys-expectations-amid-contract-increase/">KXAN</a></span>

City staff released memos the same day finding each Catholic Charities program met its performance expectations; the one compliance issue noted was difficulty reaching the clients the programs target. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/austin-funded-immigration-programs-meet-citys-expectations-amid-contract-increase/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### A Wells Branch murder-suicide turned out to be a double homicide
The Travis County Sheriff's Office said Friday that ballistics and autopsy results contradicted its first reading of two
deaths in the 3400 block of Shoreline Drive. Anecia Kay Thacker, 37, and Lamonica Webb, 32, both Austin residents, were
found shot Wednesday morning after an apartment manager called 911. Deputies charged Michael Robinson, 24, of
Vicksburg, Mississippi, with capital murder. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/crime/austin-double-homicide-arrest/">KXAN</a></span>

##### A 17-year-old is charged in Wednesday's East Austin killing
Austin police charged Jorge Garza with murder on Friday in the shooting of Jacob Villanueva, 29, inside an apartment in
the 1000 block of Springdale Road. Investigators said the woman who lived there had argued with Garza, her boyfriend,
then called Villanueva, her former boyfriend, who asked Garza to leave. The Lone Star Fugitive Task Force made the
arrest. It is Austin's 42nd homicide of 2026. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/17-year-old-arrested-charged-with-murder-in-east-austin-apartment-shooting">CBS Austin</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Council rezoned the old Rosedale School for 435 apartments
The 4.62-acre property at 2117 W. 49th St. goes from single-family to multifamily zoning with a 75-foot height limit,
approved on all three readings Thursday. Neighbors filed a valid petition, so it needed a council supermajority.
District 7 Council Member Mike Siegel added a direction to consider street impact fees for safety work at 49th Street
and Burnet Road. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/development/austin-council-gives-final-ok-to-rosedale-school-rezoning/">Community Impact</a></span>

##### Airbnb is putting $6.4 million into a city lot that has sat empty for two decades
The St. John redevelopment at 800 E. St. John Ave. is meant to hold 201 affordable units and a companion project of 325
mixed-income homes, plus retail, a park and public art. The city has owned the land since 2013. Airbnb said a funding
gap had held up construction about five years despite community support and state tax
incentives. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/airbnb-invests-6-4-million-austin-affordable-housing-project">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

##### Travis County got a year of delayed voter registrations dumped on it this week
Voter Registrar Celia Israel said the county received about 32,000 backlogged applications from the Texas Department of
Public Safety (DPS) on Tuesday, on top of 3,000 outside the backlog; FOX 7 Austin put the delayed total near 36,000.
DPS said it found the problem in late August and blamed an outside vendor whose 2025 software update cut off parts of
each record. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-25/texas-dps-voter-registration-applications-failed-to-send">KUT</a></span>

Registration for the Nov. 3 election closes Oct. 5, and early voting in Travis County runs Oct. 19 through Oct. 30; the Bulletin's <a href="/vote-2026/">Vote 2026</a> page carries the official links. <span class="src"><a href="https://votetravis.gov/current-election-information/current-election/">Travis County Elections</a></span>

Hispanic. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/education/2026-09-25/webb-middle-school-austin-tx-closure-petition-parent-meeting-aisd-takeover">KUT</a></span>

drop tower, Downforce, which drops riders 173 feet, comes from the Austrian maker a Florida jury found negligent after
14-year-old Tyre Sampson fell to his death from a similar ride in 2022. KXAN found the Austin version does not tilt,

<p class="good-thing">The Seaholm Intake Facility spent decades pumping lake water to the downtown power plant, and for most of the years since it has been a gray mid-century box that people jog past without looking up. Emily Eisenhart looked up. She was on an architectural boat tour of Lady Bird Lake three years ago when she noticed the freshly cleaned concrete catching the spring light, and thought it was "a beacon that was waiting for art." Because the building is historic, nothing permanent was possible, so she floated the idea of projecting onto it instead. That show is tonight. WaterWork, produced by the nonprofit Design Austin with the Trail Conservancy, projects two-minute films from 22 artists onto the old intake, free, starting at 8:30. The invitation is to watch from a paddleboard or a kayak, with the art coming back up off the water; if you would rather stay dry, the Pfluger Pedestrian Bridge and Auditorium Shores both look straight at it. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/life-arts/2026-09-23/austin-tx-waterwork-art-lakefront-lady-bird-lake-trail-conservancy">KUT</a></span></p>
