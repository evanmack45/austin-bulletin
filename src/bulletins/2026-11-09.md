### Register by Thursday if a runoff is ordered.

<!-- desk:kicker Bulletin original -->

A resident who still needs to register, or to change an address, for a possible December 12 city runoff has until Thursday, November 12.

{% leadfigure %}

The city's runoff calendar calls November 12 the last day to register to vote in the runoff election. It schedules the municipal canvass for 11 a.m. November 17 in the Council Chambers at City Hall, 301 W. Second Street, and says the actual canvass could come earlier once counties deliver returns. Any necessary runoff would be ordered on that canvass day. The calendar does not say a runoff has been ordered. If one is ordered, the same calendar lists November 30 as the first day of early voting by personal appearance, December 8 as the last day, and December 12 as election day.

That is not an order. The Texas Secretary of State runoff calendar sets Thursday, November 12, 2026, as the last day to register to vote or to make a change of address effective for the Saturday, December 12, 2026 runoff election. It lists early voting by personal appearance from Monday, November 30, 2026, through Tuesday, December 8, 2026, for runoff elections that result from the November 3 uniform election date.

The Travis County Clerk's current-election page lists Monday, October 5, 2026, as the last day to register to vote for the Tuesday, November 3, 2026, joint election.

##### County court meets Thursday to canvass the Nov. 3 election.
The Travis County Clerk posted a special voting session for Thursday, November 12, 2026. The agenda sets 9:00 a.m. at the Commissioners Courtroom, 700 Lavaca Street, and says a quorum will be physically present. It asks the court to consider an order canvassing returns and declaring results for the November 3, 2026, general election for federal, state, district, and county officeholders. The agenda does not say the order is approved.
