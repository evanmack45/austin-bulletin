### If the state steps in at Austin's schools, the district pays

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-12/superintendent-pay.png" alt="Bar chart of superintendent base salaries, as reported by Austin Current: Austin Independent School District Superintendent Matias Segura earns $362,000; Houston ISD&apos;s state-appointed superintendent Mike Miles was hired in 2023 at $380,000; his base pay has since been raised to $462,000.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin &middot; Austin Current</figcaption>
</figure>

The Austin Independent School District (AISD) is waiting to learn what the Texas Education Agency (TEA) will do about Webb and Burnet middle schools, which each drew five straight failing ratings. Whatever the agency orders, Austin Current reports, the district will pay for it.

TEA gives districts no extra money for intervention. Agency spokesperson Jake Kobersky said the state "works with district leadership to identify additional revenue sources."

Other districts show the range. Fort Worth paid at least $412,000 to part ways with its superintendent after a March takeover, and now pays a state-appointed conservator by the hour. The Houston Independent School District drew nearly $450 million from reserves in the first two years of state control.

Austin starts from a hole. It ended last year $105 million short, closed 11 schools, and holds about half the reserves its board policy calls for. Trustee Candace Hunter said the district cannot absorb new costs and that the state should help pay for changes.

The district has appealed the ratings. If they stand, TEA Commissioner Mike Morath can order a full takeover or appoint a conservator and leave the current leaders in place.

Until property taxes arrive, AISD is borrowing $140 million to cover payroll through November.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> AISD's appeal of the Webb and Burnet ratings is pending; TEA has not announced its plans for the district.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Austin ISD would cover the cost of any state intervention
Any changes the Texas Education Agency orders at AISD would be paid for by the district, Austin Current reports. Fort Worth's district paid at least $412,000 to its departing superintendent, and Houston's took nearly $450 million from reserves in two years. AISD Superintendent Matias Segura earns a base salary of $362,000, and his contract does not say what he would be paid if the state took over. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/12/austin-isd-state-takeover-costs/">Austin Current</a></span>

With its cash reserves low, AISD must borrow $140 million to make payroll through November while it waits for property tax payments to come in. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/12/austin-isd-state-takeover-costs/">Austin Current</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Airport braces for 40,000-plus departures after ACL
Austin-Bergstrom International Airport expects more than 40,000 passengers to depart Monday as Austin City Limits (ACL) Festival visitors head home, CBS Austin reports. A typical day sees about 25,000 to 30,000 departing passengers. It is the airport's second day in a week at that level, after last Thursday. Officials recommend arriving at least 2.5 hours before a domestic flight and three hours before an international one. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-airport-expects-more-than-40000-departing-travelers-after-acl-weekend-two">CBS Austin</a></span>

#### City Hall & county

##### Council to vote on $224 million more for airport utility work
The City Council's October 22 agenda includes item 27, which would add $224 million to the airport's utility infrastructure contract with Sundt/Archer Western, for a total of up to $344 million. Council approved the original contract, up to $120 million, in July 2024. The work covers storm drainage, water quality ponds and power distribution. Our <a href="/council/2026-10-22-preview/">October 22 Council preview</a> has two more items. <span class="src"><a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=483543">City of Austin</a></span>
