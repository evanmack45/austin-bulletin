### Today's tax vote comes without an election

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-09/central-health-bill.png" alt="Bar chart of the range Central Health gives for what its proposed property tax rate increase would add to the average Travis County homeowner&apos;s annual Central Health tax bill: 36 dollars at the low end of the proposal and 44 dollars at the high end">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · FOX 7 Austin</figcaption>
</figure>

Central Health, the hospital district that taxes property owners across Travis County, sets its 2027 rate today. The
board chooses between an increase of 6% to 8% and a "no-new-revenue" rate that would leave the average bill where it
is. State law requires an election only above a certain threshold. This proposal sits below it, so nobody votes on it
but the board.

The money has a destination. Central Health says the increase would raise $22.1 million toward inpatient behavioral
health services the district does not currently offer, and toward a reserve for a mental health crisis center.
President and chief executive Dr. Patrick Lee told the board's Sept. 2 meeting the goal is "to close those care gaps
and build a system without edges where people can enter through any door." Lago Vista Mayor Shane Saum said most
residents he talks to do not know they are in the taxing district at all. Matt Mackowiak of Save Austin Now said he
supports helping people in crisis but wants an external audit before any increase.

Austin paychecks moved this year too. The payroll company Automatic Data Processing (ADP) put median gross pay growth
in the Austin metro at 5.2% from August 2025 to August 2026, a higher rate than workers saw in Houston, Dallas-Fort
Worth or San Antonio.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Central Health's board votes on the fiscal 2027 tax rate and budget today, Sept. 9.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Central Health decides today what its share of your tax bill is
Central Health's board votes today on its fiscal 2027 tax rate, choosing between an increase of 6% to 8% and a
no-new-revenue rate. The district says the increase would bring in $22.1 million and add $36 to $44 a year to the
average homeowner's Central Health line. It also holds $18.5 million from a Texas Department of Transportation
(TxDOT) eminent domain settlement. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/central-health-vote-proposed-property-tax-increase-travis-county">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

The proposed budget would fund 22 inpatient psychiatric beds and a 60-bed mental health crisis center, with a $7.3 million reserve set aside for the center's future operations. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/central-health-vote-proposed-property-tax-increase-travis-county">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### Immigration agents took two Cisco's workers from the lot
U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents arrested two workers outside Cisco's Restaurant Bakery and Bar
early Monday at the request of the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS), a Department of Homeland Security
spokesperson said. DPS officials encountered Ramon Avilez-Frias and Maria Del Pilar Moreno-Loredo at an Austin traffic
stop and called ICE. Both were offered voluntary departure the same day and stay in custody until removal. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/article/ciscos-workers-arrested-ice-austin-22421000.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

Cisco's said on Instagram the two were long-standing employees parked for work at 6 a.m. Monday, and that it is devastated: "These are not only employees, but fathers, husbands and wives." <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/article/ciscos-workers-arrested-ice-austin-22421000.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

##### The state's own camera network keeps running
DPS has no apparent plans to stop using its roadside cameras after Gov. Greg Abbott blocked state agencies from
spending on Flock Safety equipment. An email a lawmaker posted on social media shows at least 940 DPS cameras had been
installed. A Texas Tribune review of one month of searches found about 615,000 queries in June by 3,075 agencies,
2,679 of them from outside Texas. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/after-abbotts-order-limiting-flock-cameras-dps-to-continue-using-its-vast-surveillance-network/">KXAN</a></span>

Of those June searches, more than 58,000 listed traffic infractions such as running a stop sign or following too closely, and 111 cited immigration reasons including an "ICE deportation warrant." <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/after-abbotts-order-limiting-flock-cameras-dps-to-continue-using-its-vast-surveillance-network/">KXAN</a></span>

Travis County Emergency Services District No. 1 has 15 firefighters assigned to wildfires around the state, among them crew members who spent eight days on the Ross Fire. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/travis-county-firefighters-deployed-across-texas-to-battle-wildfires/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Four Points wants to know what is going up on the old 3M campus
The city approved a permit to remodel the former 3M campus in Four Points as a technology research and development
center, but a Securities and Exchange Commission filing from SB Energy lists the project, Cosmos, among its data
centers. SB Energy says the chip company Graphcore will base its U.S. engineering headquarters there, and that phase
one carries about 50 megawatts of capacity. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/community/data-centers/project-cosmos-northwest-austin-neighbor-concerns/269-e5e79425-6d4e-44ec-95c2-2e8d7bf96628">KVUE</a></span>

Austin Development Services said site plan revisions carry no public notice requirement, so no formal public input process has happened; residents are organizing a town hall of their own. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/community/data-centers/project-cosmos-northwest-austin-neighbor-concerns/269-e5e79425-6d4e-44ec-95c2-2e8d7bf96628">KVUE</a></span>

TxDOT has reached substantial completion on Oak Hill Parkway, which rebuilt seven miles of highway into a six-lane divided road with new frontage roads and 14 miles of shared-use paths. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/southwest-austin-dripping-springs/transportation/oak-hill-parkway-reaches-substantial-completion-3-southwest-austin-dripping-springs-transportation-updates/">Community Impact</a></span>

#### Schools

##### A third campus lost its cooling, and the district is mid-cut
Lamar Middle School sent students home at 11:30 a.m. Tuesday so crews could install a temporary chiller, Principal
Travis Brunner wrote to families. It was the third Austin Independent School District (Austin ISD) campus with air
conditioning trouble in under two weeks, after McCallum High on Aug. 27 and Anderson High on Sept. 1. The district is
working through a $205 million reduction package. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/lamar-middle-school-experiences-hvac-issues-sends-students-home-early/">KXAN</a></span>

Austin Current reported the reduction package cut $1 million for Communities in Schools, which then opened a fundraiser with a $325,000 goal to keep its campus programs running. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/09/austin-austinisd-budget-cuts-nonprofits/">Austin Current</a></span>

Parents, educators and community members launched Keep Austin Schools Independent, a coalition opposing a state takeover, a day before the Texas Education Agency (TEA) released its 2025-26 accountability ratings. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/education/new-coalition-opposes-state-takeover-we-are-fighting-to-keep-austins-schools-independent/">Community Impact</a></span>

The Texas comptroller's office asked lawmakers for more than $2 billion for education savings accounts, twice the $1 billion the Legislature allocated to launch the program this school year. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/texas-legislature/comptroller-asks-lawmakers-to-double-texas-education-freedom-account-funding-to-2b/">Community Impact</a></span>

Two is a cause for concern," the letter said. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/life-arts/2026-09-08/austin-tx-red-river-cultural-district-letter-city-preservation-music-venue-closures">KUT</a></span>
