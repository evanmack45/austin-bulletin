### Monday is the registration cutoff. The rolls are still catching up.

<figure class="graphic">

Monday is the last day to register to vote in the Nov. 3 election. Travis County is still working through more than 35,000
registration records the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) never forwarded, about 32,000 of which landed at the Tax
Office in one overnight batch on Sept. 22 and 23. Some were filed nearly a year ago.

DPS points to a vendor. The software that handles its daily transfers to the Secretary of State was updated in October 2025
and began cutting off parts of records, the agency said; it found the problem in late August and fixed it on Sept. 13. What
that means for a voter is narrower than it sounds: a registration counts from the day of the DPS transaction, so anyone who
registered that way by Monday is eligible even if the county has not processed the record yet. A provisional ballot is the
backstop.

The candidates are behind on their own paperwork too. Eight Austin City Council candidates missed the Aug. 24 deadline for
the city's financial disclosure form and seven missed the Sept. 8 state one, Community Impact's Ben Thompson reported, with
candidates in all five races among them. The Ethics Review Commission told him it happens election after election. On this
ballot: five council seats, a bond election, and Proposition I, which would require an independent efficiency audit of every
city department every five years.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Registration closes Monday, early voting opens Oct. 19, and Election Day is Nov. 3. The Bulletin's <a href="/vote-2026/">Vote 2026</a> page carries the official state and county links; Saturday's registration drives are in <a href="#city-hall-county">City Hall &amp; county</a>.</p>

#### Public safety & courts

##### U.S. 183 shut in both directions near the airport before dawn
Both directions of U.S. 183 between William Cannon Drive and FM 812, near Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, were
closed early Saturday while officials responded to what CBS Austin described as a serious collision. The Travis County
Sheriff's Office said delays would last several hours. The closure was reported shortly before 5 a.m. Nothing has been said
yet about how many vehicles were involved, or whether anyone was hurt. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/serious-crash-shuts-down-us-183-in-both-directions-near-austin-airport">CBS Austin</a></span>

##### Austin Municipal Court moves every jury trial to a Tuesday, starting Monday
Austin Municipal Court is consolidating its calendar beginning Oct. 5: all attorney appearance dockets and jury trials will
be heard on Tuesdays. The court said it reviewed its docket times, found some underused, and expects the change to free
staff and make room for other dockets it needs. Walk-in mitigation hours at the Burleson Road courthouse are
unchanged. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/austin-municipal-court-streamlines-docket-schedule">City of Austin</a></span>

The Austin Police Department (APD) arrested David Benjamin Coleman-Roush, 35, early Friday in North Austin on a
terroristic-threat charge over threats against the University of Texas and a church. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/crime/man-arrested-for-making-threats-against-university-of-texas-local-church/">KXAN</a></span>

University of Texas Police Chief Shane Streepy said Coleman-Roush has no affiliation with the university and was reportedly
suffering from a mental health crisis when he was arrested. <span class="src"><a href="https://thedailytexan.com/2026/10/02/apd-arrests-man-for-terroristic-threat-against-ut/">The Daily Texan</a></span>

APD executed arrest warrants against four men on Sept. 24, in an operation it called Protect Our Parks, built on crime data
from around Barking Springs at Barton Springs Pool. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/apd-south-metro-tactical-unit-announces-multiple-arrests-operation-protect-our-parks">City of Austin</a></span>

The department said reports in that area between Jan. 1 and June 9 included aggravated robbery, vehicle burglary and
