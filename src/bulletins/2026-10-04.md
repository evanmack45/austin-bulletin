### The festival has to put Zilker back

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-04/zilker-restoration.png" alt="Timeline of the 2026 Austin City Limits Music Festival at Zilker Park. The first weekend runs October 2 through 4. On October 3 the gates opened at 2 p.m. instead of noon because of wet grounds. The second weekend runs October 9 through 11. The Parks Department says the festival contract requires restoration of the Great Lawn and surrounding areas after the event, including replacement grass where needed; no reopening date is promised here.">
<figcaption>Graphic: The Austin Bulletin &middot; KXAN · FOX 7 Austin · City of Austin</figcaption>
</figure>

Saturday's mud at Zilker Park became a question about who restores the lawn once the music leaves. Austin City Limits (ACL) Music Festival delayed its gates from noon to 2 p.m. while crews worked on the wet grounds. The park still has another festival weekend ahead.

Restoration is part of the contract. The city's Parks Department told KXAN that the festival must restore the Great Lawn and surrounding areas, including replacement grass where needed. Organizer C3 Presents said it remediates Zilker every year. Crews were removing standing water and putting down mulch; the Parks Department said all of that mulch will come out afterward and damaged sod will be replaced.

Council Member Ryan Alter called the damage unacceptable and asked for clearer maintenance policies. He told KXAN he did not think the city's deal needed revision and was confident the park would be restored. CBS Austin separately reported his concerns and festivalgoers' difficulty walking across the lawn.

The promise is a repaired public park. Neither account supplies a firm date when the Great Lawn will reopen for ordinary use.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The second weekend runs Oct. 9–11, with restoration following the festival and no firm lawn reopening date in the reports.</p>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### The lawn repair belongs to the festival
The Parks Department says the festival’s contract requires restoration of Zilker’s Great Lawn and surrounding areas, including replacement grass where needed. The Parks Department said crews were using mulch to make muddy areas passable and removing standing water. After the festival, all the mulch will be removed and damaged sod replaced, the Parks Department told KXAN. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/acl/acl-festivals-muddy-great-lawn-raises-concern-for-austin-parks-future/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Schools

##### The district is hiring the people behind the school day
Austin Independent School District (AISD) held a support staff hiring fair Saturday at its headquarters, with about 70 openings in transportation, groundskeeping, custodial work and child nutrition. Managers conducted interviews on the spot and could recommend applicants for hiring. People who missed the fair can still apply through the district’s careers website, talent officer Woodrow Bailey told KXAN. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/austin-isd-hosts-hiring-fair-to-fill-support-staff-positions/">KXAN</a></span>
