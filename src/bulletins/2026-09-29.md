high is 91 and by Friday it is 84, with rain most of both days. Sun up at 7:23, down at 7:18. The air is clean and the lake

### Zilker is clearing its drains

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-29/rain-chance-week.png" alt="Bar chart of the National Weather Service daytime chance of precipitation for Austin, from the Austin-San Antonio forecast office grid point for central Austin, issued the morning of September 29, 2026. <figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin &middot; National Weather Service</figcaption>

Thursday is the day. The National Weather Service put it at a 100% chance of rain in Austin, with Friday at 90% and the
afternoon high falling from 97 today to 84 by Friday. Parts of Central Texas are under a Level 3 of 4 flash flood
risk. Gov. Greg Abbott held a briefing Monday at the State Operations Center and said Texas has staged 1,900 responders and
more than 1,500 pieces of equipment, among them high-water vehicles, rescue boats and helicopters. "We are going to face a
heavy rain incident like we did this past July," he said.

The Austin City Limits (ACL) Music Festival opens Friday at Zilker Park. Organizers told KXAN they are clearing drains,
pre-positioning equipment and moving the build-out schedule up to protect the park.

Out in western Travis County, the Sandy Creek Alliance goes before Commissioners Court this morning. Its members want the
county to install River Sentry sirens instead of the system it is weighing, which they say will not wake a sleeping house.
Resident Ashlee Willis said the July 2025 flood stripped the creek of trees and foliage: an inch of rain there now brings a
foot of rise.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Travis County Commissioners Court hears the Sandy Creek Alliance this morning, and the county's siren plan is due to the Texas Water Development Board by Dec. 31. #### Money & bills

##### CapMetro's board voted the fare increase through
Directors approved the first system-wide fare change since 2015 at Monday's meeting. A local bus or Pickup ride goes from
$1.25 to $1.50 in January 2027 and $2 in January 2029. Commuter service goes to $4 then $5, and Access, the paratransit
service, to $2.25 then $3.25. The agency cited rising costs of fuel, equipment, maintenance and transit
operations. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/traffic/transportation/capmetro-directors-consider-fare-increase-monday/">KXAN</a></span>

Discounts for seniors, people with disabilities, Medicare card holders and eligible military personnel stay in place under the approved change, the agency said. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/traffic/transportation/capmetro-directors-consider-fare-increase-monday/">KXAN</a></span>

City Council signed off on $4.13 million in social service contract extensions on Sept. 24, keeping public health, housing, legal aid and food access programs running while staff evaluations continue. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/council-approves-4-1m-in-social-service-contract-extensions-amid-ongoing-reviews/">Community Impact</a></span>

Council Member Ryan Alter voted against a $350,000 extension for the Sickle Cell Texas Marc Thomas Foundation, which an internal report faulted for inflated performance reporting and unallowable costs. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/council-approves-4-1m-in-social-service-contract-extensions-amid-ongoing-reviews/">Community Impact</a></span>

Mayor Kirk Watson voted against most of the extensions, saying council needed the analysis it had asked for first. A memo with detailed staff evaluations circulated hours after the vote. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/council-approves-4-1m-in-social-service-contract-extensions-amid-ongoing-reviews/">Community Impact</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### A 19-year-old is charged in the crash that killed Miles Longbine
Austin police arrested King Anthony Henry, 19, on a charge of driving while intoxicated as a minor over a Sept. 12
single-vehicle crash at Northwest Park, 7000 Ardath Street. Officers were called about 9:30 p.m. Longbine, also 19, was
taken to a hospital and died at 10:32 p.m. Police count it as Austin's 59th fatal crash of the
year. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/19-year-old-arrested-accused-of-dwi-in-deadly-north-austin-crash/">KXAN</a></span>

found shot in a north Travis County apartment on Sept. 23 by a manager coming to evict them. Deputies first called
it a murder-suicide. Robinson told investigators he fired after both women drew guns on
him. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/crime/affidavit-austin-man-killed-two-women-during-visit-to-pay-for-sex/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Sandy Creek wants a louder siren than the one the county is buying
Members of the Sandy Creek Alliance speak at Travis County Commissioners Court this morning, asking the county to choose
River Sentry sirens for its flood warning network. Resident Ashlee Willis said that model runs at 90 decibels, above the
Texas Water Development Board minimum. The county plans to install sirens by August 2027 under Texas Senate Bill 3, and
owes the board its plan by Dec. 31. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/sandy-creek-residents-ask-travis-county-for-river-sentry-flood-sirens">CBS Austin</a></span>

Resident Joseph Shopp said the alliance is not arguing about cost: "We are concerned about the lives and the livelihoods of those people along the river." <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/sandy-creek-residents-ask-travis-county-for-river-sentry-flood-sirens">CBS Austin</a></span>

#### Schools

##### Burnet and Webb parents have until Oct. 15 to ask the state to close their own schools
Superintendent Matias Segura told about four dozen Burnet families on Monday evening that a parent petition is their last
chance to shape what happens to the campus. Both middle schools drew a fifth preliminary failing rating, which obliges
Education Commissioner Mike Morath to intervene once the ratings are final. A petition needs signatures from parents of more than half the enrolled
students. Neither community has started one. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/28/austin-district-takeover-petition-webb-burnet/">Austin Current</a></span>

#### Business & street life

##### Taqueria Chapala served its last plate on East Cesar Chavez after 29 years
Manuel Lopez and his brother opened at 2101 E. Cesar Chavez St. on July 1, 1997. They closed Sept. 27 after the landlords
declined to renew the lease, and plan to reopen Oct. 29 at 2448 S. First St. A sign now offers the old space as a
second-generation restaurant. "We've worked here for practically our entire lives," Mauricio Lopez
said. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/east-austin/dining/taqueria-chapala-closes-on-east-cesar-chavez-to-reopen-on-south-first/">Community Impact</a></span>

Manuel Lopez wrote that the landlords handed him a document on May 1 stating they would not renew, and that he did not read it: "That was my mistake." <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/east-austin/dining/taqueria-chapala-closes-on-east-cesar-chavez-to-reopen-on-south-first/">Community Impact</a></span>
