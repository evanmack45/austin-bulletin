### Austin has started counting what the heat does

![Bar chart of heat-related emergency room visits in Austin and Travis County: 1,355 visits in 2023, when 34 people died; 937 visits in 2025, when 21 people died; and 880 visits so far in 2026, with no deaths posted by Austin Public Health](/images/2026-08-30/heat-er-visits.png)
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Austin Public Health, via the Austin American-Statesman</figcaption>

Austin Public Health has put a heat dashboard online, and the summer now has figures attached to it. Area emergency
rooms have treated 880 heat-related cases since May, among them 43 heat strokes and 261 cases of dehydration. Medics
have answered 173 heat calls.

The deaths are the figure that moves. In 2023, a summer with 80 triple-digit days, emergency rooms here saw 1,355
heat cases and 34 people died. Last year 21 died. Austin has had 30 days at or above 100 this year, and the
department has posted no deaths at all.

The dashboard also says when the danger arrives. Six in the evening is the worst hour of the day, weekdays are worse
than weekends, and Sunday is the mildest day on the chart.

Heat lands hardest on people without a door to close. A cooling center in the basement of St. David's Episcopal
Church, run by Trinity Center, has taken thousands of visits since June and opens four afternoons a week. Dr. Ann
Buchanan, an emergency room physician at St. David's HealthCare, said what she mostly sees is heat exhaustion:
"True heat stroke is actually pretty rare."

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Austin Public Health updates the dashboard through October, and the National Weather Service has Austin near 100 again Monday and Tuesday before the <a href="#weather">week cools</a>.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Central Health asks for a rate above no-new-revenue, and votes Wednesday
The Travis County Healthcare District, which does business as Central Health, has proposed a 2026 tax rate of
$0.135195 per $100 of value. Its no-new-revenue rate is $0.118633, so the proposal raises property taxes. The public
hearing and the vote are set for Wednesday at 5:30 p.m. at 1111 E. Cesar Chavez St. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.traviscountytx.gov/news/2026/2938-notice-hearing-vote-tax-increase">Travis County</a></span>

Travis County has published its own 2026 rates: a no-new-revenue rate of $0.378179 per $100 and a voter-approval rate of $0.386210, with $515.5 million unencumbered in the general fund. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.traviscountytx.gov/news/2026/2931-notice-about-2026-tax-rate">Travis County</a></span>

El Buen Samaritano has hired the navigators for the city's expanded displacement prevention program, working Dove Springs, Montopolis, Colony Park and households near Project Connect corridors through March 2027. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/housing/news/navigators-selected-expanded-displacement-prevention-navigator-program">City of Austin</a></span>

Trinity Center's cooling center in a downtown church basement runs 1 to 7 p.m. Monday through Thursday, on top of the year-round services it keeps from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. weekdays. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/article/austin-cooling-center-homeless-summer-heat-22407596.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

Austin's parks carry about $700 million in overdue repairs, and neighborhood projects increasingly lean on outside money; a parks bond goes to voters Nov. 3. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-parks-rely-on-outside-funding-as-maintenance-needs-reach-700-million">CBS Austin</a></span>

##### A Statesman review backs part of the U.S. attorney's crime claim, not all of it
Justin R. Simmons, the U.S. attorney for the Western District of Texas, said this week that his office had filed
about 40 federal gun cases in Austin in recent months and that since 2015 homicides are up 187 percent, aggravated
assault 38 percent and rape 65 percent. The Austin American-Statesman reviewed the data and reported that the rape
figure is not supported, and that the answer depends on raw totals or rates. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/news/courts/article/austin-violent-crime-rates-data-trends-22403622.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### The city will cut 37 trees to save the 1917 wall at Barton Springs
Austin Parks and Recreation starts work Sept. 8 on the stone retaining wall along the eastern edge of Barton Springs
Pool, built in 1917 and part of what makes Zilker Park a historic district. Five sections are failing. Crews will
remove 37 trees whose roots are inside the wall, mostly hackberry, then clear vines for an engineer's assessment.
The pool keeps normal hours. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/parks/news/retaining-wall-repair-and-tree-removal-barton-springs-pool">City of Austin</a></span>

## Weather

Today: sunny, with a high near 100 and heat index values as high as 103, on a south wind of 0 to 5 mph. There are no
active alerts for Travis County. Tonight: mostly clear with a low around 77. Monday and Tuesday are both sunny and
near 100. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.weather.gov/ewx/">National Weather Service</a></span>

High pressure slides east this week and loosens its hold, which the National Weather Service and KXAN both expect to
mean more days in the 90s than in the triple digits — with humidity climbing to muggy by midweek and a 10 percent
rain chance Thursday. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/weather/forecast/more-90s-than-triple-digits-this-week/">KXAN</a></span>

If Austin reaches 100 today as forecast, the current run of triple-digit days moves nearer the city's ten longest
streaks on record. August has produced 25 of this year's 100-degree days; July produced five. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.statesman.com/weather/article/austin-weather-hottest-august-ever-temperatures-22406703.php">Austin American-Statesman</a></span>
