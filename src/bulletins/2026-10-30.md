### Last day of early voting: 41.98%.

<!-- desk:kicker Election · Early voting -->
<!-- desk:figure 41.98% -->
<!-- desk:photo courthouse -->

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-30/early-ballots.png" alt="Bar chart from Travis County unofficial early voting totals through Oct. 29: 398,190 ballots cast and 948,575 registered voters.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County</figcaption>
</figure>

Through Thursday, Oct. 29, 398,190 people had voted, 41.98% of the county's 948,575 registered voters, according to the county's unofficial daily totals. That count is 379,696 in-person early votes, 2,313 limited ballots and 16,181 mail ballots received. Thursday brought 37,831 voters, the most of any day this week.

<p class="if-you">Friday, Oct. 30, is the last day of early voting, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. at any county site, with seven sites open until 10 p.m. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., and you can vote at any polling site in Travis County. <a href="https://votetravis.gov/current-election-information/current-election/">Vote Travis election page</a>.</p>

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3.</p>

#### Public safety & courts

##### Nov. 5: try a drunk-driving simulator at City Hall.
Austin Municipal Court marks Municipal Court Week, Nov. 2-6, with a traffic safety event Thursday, Nov. 5, run with Texas A&M AgriLife Extension's Watch UR BAC program. The court is also starting a series of short videos on its services.

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### 7,091 grass carp go into Lake Austin to eat hydrilla.
<!-- desk:week Oct 29 -->
Austin Watershed Protection released 7,091 sterile grass carp into Lake Austin on Thursday, Oct. 29, near Jessica Hollis Park, Walsh Boat Ramp and Bull Creek, where hydrilla, a non-native water plant, is thickest. A Texas Parks and Wildlife survey in mid-September estimated 575 acres of hydrilla, and the city says coverage has held steady since June. The stocking number comes from a new city model built on 15 years of Lake Austin data.
<p class="if-you"><span class="date-tag">Oct 29</span> The city asks boaters to clean, drain and dry boats, trailers and gear, and asks lakeside residents to cut back on fertilizer.</p>
<span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/watershed-protection/news/austin-watershed-protection-stocks-more-grass-carp-lake-austin-help-0">Austin Watershed Protection release</a></span>

#### City Hall & county

##### 87%: this week's in-person turnout trails last week's.
<!-- desk:closes Oct 30 -->
Monday through Thursday of this week, 137,656 people voted in person at county sites, 87% of the 158,400 who voted on the same four days of the first week, Oct. 19-22.
<p class="if-you"><span class="date-tag">Oct 30</span> Limited ballots went the other way: 1,580 this Monday through Thursday, up from 332 in the first four days, and Thursday's 583 were the most of any day so far. Ben Hur Shrine Center has drawn the most in-person voters of any site, 16,116, followed by Bee Cave City Hall at 15,427. The seven sites open until 10 p.m. Friday are Ben Hur Shrine Center, Lakeway Activity Center, Millennium Youth Entertainment Complex, PfISD Rock Gym, Randalls Brodie, the Travis County Clerk's office on Airport Boulevard and the UT Texas Union.</p>
