### Before the shooting there was a month of stops, at gas stations and outside the Dollar Tree

A U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officer shot Wilber Rafael Garces Perez, 28, on Anderson Square on
Sunday afternoon while he was out on a DoorDash run. Monday filled in the rest. Juan Perez, who works at a Mexican
restaurant near that intersection, told Austin Current that officers had spent a month picking up construction workers
at the gas station and outside the Dollar Tree. Mayor Kirk Watson put a figure on it: detentions and contacts in Austin
went from a number you could count on one hand to the thirties, in about a week, with no ramp-up before it. Council
Member Chito Vela, whose district covers the site, called the enforcement random.

Then the city met its limits. Eight council members have called for answers. The Travis County District
Attorney's office asked to join as a co-equal partner and for access to federal evidence. All of that depends on whether
federal officials hand anything over, and Homeland Security Investigations, with the Federal Bureau of Investigation, is
running a review of its own.

Garces Perez spent Monday in a detention center in Pearsall, two hours southwest of here, calling into an Austin news
conference by phone. His attorney says the bullet is still in his back.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Homeland Security Investigations and the Federal Bureau of Investigation have opened a review, and the Austin Police Department's Special Investigations Unit and the Texas Rangers are investigating alongside it; city officials have asked for body and dash camera footage to be released. More in <a href="#public-safety-courts">Public safety &amp; courts</a>.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Abbott froze every data center permit in Texas until the grid audit is done
Gov. Greg Abbott ordered the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) on Monday to stop issuing permits for data
center projects until the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) finishes its audit of how the industry uses
power and water. The Public Utility Commission of Texas and the Texas Water Development Board are running
reviews alongside it. TCEQ must report back by Oct. 19. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/texas-pausing-permits-all-data-centers">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

Developers with pending projects must disclose whether they will generate their own electricity or draw from the grid, plus projected power and water use, cooling technology, tax subsidies and ownership. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/texas-pausing-permits-all-data-centers">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

Abbott said he will work with lawmakers in the next legislative session to eliminate financial incentives for data centers in Texas. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/texas/abbott-orders-halt-to-texas-data-center-permits-pending-grid-water-audits/">KXAN</a></span>

##### Travis County's next budget goes to a final vote a week from today
Commissioners are scheduled to adopt it Sept. 29. They approved a tax rate 2.5% higher last week, but the owner of a
median-valued home, worth $372,633, is expected to see a roughly $30 decrease in the county portion of the tax bill,
because the median taxable value fell. Judge Andy Brown said counties are asked to do more with limited
resources. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/22/texas-travis-county-property-tax-budget/">Austin Current</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### The man shot by an ICE officer called an Austin news conference from a cell in Pearsall
Wilber Rafael Garces Perez spoke by phone on Monday afternoon from the South Texas ICE Processing Center, about two
hours southwest of Austin. His attorney, Kate Lincoln-Goldfinch, translated. He said the bullet had not been removed
because it sat close to his spine, that he was in a lot of pain, and that he had been held in a very cold room without a
bed. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/ice-shooting-victim-speaks/">KXAN</a></span>

Lincoln-Goldfinch said an unmarked vehicle sideswiped her client twice, that no lights or siren came on until after the collision, and that he had asked to pull over. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/ice-shooting-victim-speaks/">KXAN</a></span>

The Department of Homeland Security said Garces Perez entered the country illegally, had a final order of removal and is in federal custody pending removal. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/ice-shooting-victim-speaks/">KXAN</a></span>

##### Four people were arrested and three officers hurt as a protest closed the intersection
Hundreds gathered on Sunday evening at the Research Boulevard service road and Anderson Square. Austin police said some
in the crowd threw rocks, bottles and pieces of concrete, damaging three police vehicles. Troopers with the Texas
Department of Public Safety used pepper ball projectiles and cleared the intersection at about 10:15 p.m. Police
arrested three men; troopers arrested a
fourth. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/3-arrested-during-north-austin-protest-after-man-shot-by-ice-officer-apd-says">CBS Austin</a></span>

Police named Robert Padgett, 48, Armando Menagarcia, 29, and Matthew Hemby, 44, on charges including riot; troopers arrested Kevin Howard Davis, 60, on a charge of obstructing a highway. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/3-arrested-during-north-austin-protest-after-man-shot-by-ice-officer-apd-says">CBS Austin</a></span>

About 100 people gathered at the front gates of the Texas Capitol on Monday evening, and the demonstration stayed peaceful under a heavy law enforcement presence. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-protests-against-ice-resume-after-agent-shoots-venezuelan-national">CBS Austin</a></span>

U.S. Rep. Lloyd Doggett said protests built on throwing rocks and venting anger do not help, and asked people to carry the protest to the polls instead. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-protests-against-ice-resume-after-agent-shoots-venezuelan-national">CBS Austin</a></span>

Council Member Mike Siegel said the city should demand a local prosecution, and that Austin police have the authority to investigate a suspected crime. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/21/texas-austin-ice-dhs-immigration-shooting/">Austin Current</a></span>

Mathew Silverman of the Federal Law Enforcement Officers Association said federal agencies are not legally required to tell local police about operations in town. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/reported-ice-surge-in-austin-addressed-at-shooting-press-conference/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Environmental groups sued to undo the Dog's Head deal and the $1 billion behind it
The Save Our Springs Alliance, People Organized in Defense of Earth and Her Resources (PODER) and several neighbors sued
the City of Austin and its council on Monday. They want a court to void the 45-year development agreement for the
2,600-acre southeast Austin project and the tax zone that would reimburse the developer more than $1 billion over 35
years. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/21/dogs-head-lawsuit-seeks-end-to-development-agreement-and-financing-plan/">Austin Current</a></span>

Bobby Levinski of the Save Our Springs Alliance said the approval was rushed and skipped the boards, commissions and notifications a normal process uses. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-21/austin-tx-dogs-head-development-sos-poder-lawsuit-amazon">KUT</a></span>

Lee Edwards of the South East Colorado River Neighborhood Association said neighbors heard about the project two days before the May vote, and heard it from a friend. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-21/austin-tx-dogs-head-development-sos-poder-lawsuit-amazon">KUT</a></span>

The suit says the agreement unconstitutionally delegates the council's legislative authority, and that the land does not qualify as unproductive or blighted under the state law the financing relies on. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/21/dogs-head-lawsuit-seeks-end-to-development-agreement-and-financing-plan/">Austin Current</a></span>

The city had not been served as of Monday evening, spokesperson Jenny LaCoste-Caputo said, and will respond through the court process. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/21/dogs-head-lawsuit-seeks-end-to-development-agreement-and-financing-plan/">Austin Current</a></span>

Amazon is expected to be the site's largest tenant and plans to anchor its robotics division there. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-21/austin-tx-dogs-head-development-sos-poder-lawsuit-amazon">KUT</a></span>

Travis County Sheriff's Office on Saturday. She said videos posted to Patrick's X account showed a version of her
describing policies she does not hold, and that a 2019 state law bars deepfakes meant to deceive
voters. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-09-21/vikki-goodwin-dan-patrick-ai-deepfake-campaign-ads-texas-lieutenant-governor-race">KUT</a></span>

The law covers the last 30 days of an election. Early voting begins Oct. 19 and Election Day is Nov. 3; the two posts Goodwin linked have since been deleted. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/texas-politics/lt-gov-candidate-goodwin-reports-dan-patrick-social-media-deepfakes-to-travis-county-sheriff/">KXAN</a></span>
