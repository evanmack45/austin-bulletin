## The Big Story

{% bigstory %}
### Two records in two days: how hot this August got, and when it lets up

![Forecast highs for the next seven days, all above 100 degrees](/images/2026-08-23/heat-week-ahead.png)
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Data: National Weather Service</figcaption>

Austin set or tied a daily heat record at both of its official stations on
Saturday and did it again on Sunday. Camp Mabry reached 107 on Saturday, tying
the Aug. 22 record set in 2024, and Austin-Bergstrom hit 107, breaking its
record of 105 from the same year, according to the National Weather Service. On
Sunday, Camp Mabry reached 107 again — a new record for Aug. 23, beating the 106
set in 2010 — and Bergstrom tied its 2010 record of 105.

The records cap a month that was already unusual. Through Aug. 20, Austin had
recorded 20 days at 100 degrees or hotter this August, with an average high of
101.4, and the first three weeks ranked as the seventh-warmest August on record,
the Austin American-Statesman reported. FOX 7 Austin counted 29 days without
measurable rain. A KXAN meteorologist quoted by KUT put the cause plainly: "All
sunshine, not much clouds, not much wind to mix things up, so it just tends to be
sort of stagnant, and that lets it get hotter."

The systems that carry the city through a stretch like this are holding. ERCOT's
forecast peak for Monday is about 90,100 megawatts against roughly 110,400
available, and Lake Travis sits at 97 percent full. What changes this week is the
alert level, not the temperature: the Extreme Heat Warning was extended through
7 p.m. Monday, and a Heat Advisory follows through 7 p.m. Wednesday, with highs
up to 105. Forecasters see the first chance of scattered showers Thursday and
Friday as the high-pressure ridge shifts west, and the Statesman notes the
Climate Prediction Center's September outlook leans wetter than normal for
Texas.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The Heat Advisory runs through 7 p.m. Wednesday. The first rain chance is Thursday. The Statesman expects one more week of triple digits before a possible break in early September.</p>

#### Roads & transit

CapMetro's Red Line is closed Aug. 24–27 for track work at the future North Burnet/Uptown station; a temporary shuttle and several bus routes cover riders during the four days. <span class="src">Statesman</span>

#### Public safety & courts

Austin and Pflugerville firefighters fought a large fire Sunday evening along East U.S. 290 near the Austin Country Flea Market, with a smoke plume visible from Interstate 35 and heavy delays on eastbound 290. KXAN reported that two people were injured; fire officials had not said what burned or how it started. <span class="src">FOX 7 Austin, KXAN</span>

#### City Hall & county

State administrative law judges have recommended that the Public Utility Commission deny the Bell County East to Big Hill 765-kilovolt line, a nearly 200-mile Oncor and LCRA project crossing Burnet, Williamson, Mason and Llano counties, finding the utilities missed some rules and did not show the line is needed. Oncor said it strongly disagrees and that the evidence of need "has only continued to grow"; the recommendation does not bind the commission, which must rule by late September. <span class="src">KVUE</span>

Lake Austin will be lowered about 10 feet from Oct. 12 to Nov. 30, its first drawdown since 2017, at the city's request and carried out by the LCRA, after a long debate over hydrilla. <span class="src">KXAN</span>

Hutto Fire Rescue said Saturday it will build Fire Station 6 on the east side of Hutto along County Road 132, near County Road 134; the district operates four stations and serves an estimated 73,000 people. <span class="src">KXAN</span>

Power is back on at the Mueller Regional Retail Center after someone stole copper service conductors; Austin Energy said at least 10 businesses were affected and service was restored by Saturday afternoon. <span class="src">KVUE</span>

Austin candle maker Akriti Marwaha, who left a software engineering job to run her handmade candle business, said her coconut-and-apricot-wax candles are about to reach H-E-B shelves. <span class="src">KXAN</span>
