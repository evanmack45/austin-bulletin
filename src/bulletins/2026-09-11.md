### Three road-death notices in one day, in a year running behind last year

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-11/traffic-deaths-pace.png" alt="Bar chart comparing Austin traffic deaths at the same point in the year, from Austin Police Department fatality releases: 75 deaths by early September 2025 and 57 deaths by early September 2026">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Austin Police Department fatality releases</figcaption>
</figure>

The Austin Police Department (APD) published three traffic fatality notices on Thursday. Each one ends with a sentence
almost nobody reads: a count of this year against the last.

The deaths came first. Lilliana Segura was nine, a passenger in a Jeep Compass that collided with a Tesla Cybertruck
where the Interstate 35 service road meets the East Ben White Boulevard service road, at 7:41 a.m. Wednesday. She died
at a hospital. The other child in the Jeep is still in critical condition, and APD's Vehicular Homicide Unit is working
out what happened. John Michael Filley was walking in the 13300 block of North Farm-to-Market 620 just after midnight on
Sept. 5 when a vehicle struck him; he died Thursday. The driver stayed at the scene and was not impaired. Kim Bao Hoang
was riding a motorcycle north on the Capital of Texas Highway at 10:49 p.m. on Sept. 4, failed to hold a curve, and hit
a guardrail.

Now the sentence. By this date in 2025, Austin had recorded 71 fatal crashes and 75 deaths. Filley's is the city's 57th
fatal crash of 2026, and its 57th death.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Austin Water sounds the Longhorn Dam floodgate warning sirens for five minutes at 10 a.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Diesel set another record in Austin, and a pickup tank now runs $190
The average gallon of diesel in Austin reached about $5.63 on Friday morning, the American Automobile Association (AAA)
reported, against $5.67 statewide and more than $6 nationally. A year ago the Austin average was about $3.24. Filling a
Ford F-250's 34-gallon tank costs roughly $190 today and would have cost about $110 last September. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/diesel-hits-record-high-in-austin-as-prices-surge-nationwide">CBS Austin</a></span>

##### County law enforcement asks commissioners for a $10 million technology contract
The Travis County Sheriff's Office, county park rangers and county constables want the county's $4.58 million contract
with Axon Enterprise lifted to $10 million in the fiscal year 2027 budget. The agencies said 1,100 jail cameras must be
replaced by the end of 2026 and that federal rules force them to retire Chinese-made drones. The Coalition for Budget
Accountability rallied against the increase on Thursday. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/travis-county/10m-contract-with-axon-proposed/">KXAN</a></span>

Redfin reported that 82.3% of Austin homes sold below their original asking price in August, the highest share of the Texas metros it listed, with a median sale price of $413,543. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/housing/report-texas-home-buyers-score-discounts-in-august/">KXAN</a></span>

City Council pulled a $5 million package of 17 consulting contracts from Thursday's agenda, the second withdrawal this year; Council Member Mike Siegel called it a victory for transparency and accountability. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/austin-pulls-millions-in-consulting-contracts-from-council-vote-for-second-time-this-year/">Community Impact</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Near the future rail stations, the affordable units have to be in the building
City Council voted unanimously on Thursday to close the fee-in-lieu option for projects using the citywide density bonus
program inside the Equitable Transit Oriented District, so a developer seeking extra height near a Project Connect
station must now put the affordable homes on site. A second item steers money already collected toward households
earning 30% of the median family income. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/11/austin-project-connect-affordable-housing-development/">Austin Current</a></span>

Awais Azhar of HousingWorks said mobility costs matter for low-income households and affordable homes should sit near transit. Greg Anderson of the Austin Housing Coalition said the requirement risks no units at all. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/11/austin-project-connect-affordable-housing-development/">Austin Current</a></span>

The Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) drawdown of Lake Austin would cost about 200 jobs and $2.5 million over six weeks, Mozart's co-owner Ken Leonard told Council on Thursday. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/business-owners-bring-lake-austin-drawdown-concerns-to-city-council">CBS Austin</a></span>
