### Five housing projects, a copper-theft request, and a school food award

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-23/city-releases.png" alt="Timeline from City of Austin releases: on October 20 the school district was recognized at Bowie High School; on October 21 police asked for tips on copper thefts near Mueller and 51st Street; on October 22 the City said Council and the Austin Housing Finance Corporation approved funding for five affordable rental developments.">
<figcaption>Timeline: The Austin Bulletin &middot; City of Austin</figcaption>
</figure>

Three City releases.

The City of Austin says the Austin City Council and the Austin Housing Finance Corporation approved more than $21 million on Oct. 22 for five affordable rental developments: Rowen Vale, The Lenora, The Maven, Jordan II and Austin Art Village. Rowen Vale, 64 units at 206 E. Annie St. in District 9, got $4.32 million and sits within a quarter mile of a planned light rail stop. The Lenora, 39 units at 4507 Menchaca Road in District 5, got $2.64 million. The City says this round includes units for households at or below 30% of median family income.

Separately, the Austin Police Department (APD) Commercial Burglary Unit says thieves have broken into electrical boxes and transformers in the Mueller and 51st Street area in recent weeks, cutting and stealing copper wire and disrupting businesses. Detectives want information about a black Dodge Ram 1500 pickup that may be connected. Tips go to APDCommercialburglary@austintexas.gov or Capital Area Crime Stoppers at 512-472-8477, with a reward of up to $1,000.

Austin Independent School District (AISD) was recognized Oct. 20 at Bowie High School by the Center for Good Food Purchasing, with national "Good Food Leader: Silver" status. The City says the district's 2024-25 assessment found 18.4% of its food spending, or $2.6 million, went to local and community-based suppliers.
