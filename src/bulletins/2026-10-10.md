### Before two new bond votes, a look at what the last ones bought

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-10/bathhouse-cost.png" alt="Bar chart of the Barton Springs bathhouse rehabilitation's cost, as reported by KUT: city staff estimated about $9.3 million in early 2023; the lower construction bid was $16.9 million; the City Council approved an $18.6 million contract; total appropriations including design and exhibits now stand at $21.98 million.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin &middot; KUT News, from City of Austin records</figcaption>
</figure>

Austin voters will decide on $295 million in parks and library bonds on November 3. This week, two reports showed how earlier bond money has fared.

The City launched an Austin Bond Dashboard on October 1 covering its 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022 programs, about $2.45 billion in voter-approved funding, CBS Austin reported. The counts differ. KXAN tallied 66 canceled projects out of 1,430; a City memo cited by CBS Austin lists 61 of 1,426. The City says money from canceled projects has been or is being reallocated.

Then there is the Barton Springs bathhouse. KUT reports that staff estimated about $9.3 million in early 2023, partly from 2012 and 2018 bonds. Total appropriations now stand at $21.98 million. Construction was due to be substantially complete by October 25, 2025. The City says the contractor, SpawGlass, has been in non-excusable delay since then; SpawGlass called the City's account incomplete. The rotunda is still closed, and no grand opening is scheduled.

On Thursday, the City Council directed staff to plan the next comprehensive bond elections for 2028 and 2034, Community Impact reported. Amounts and projects have not been set.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Early voting begins October 19; the $260 million parks bond and $35 million library bond are on the November 3 ballot.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Four of five local tax rates on Austin bills went up
Austin homeowners in Travis County and Austin Independent School District (AISD) will see five taxing entities on bills this fall, Community Impact reports. The City, Travis County, Central Health and Austin Community College raised rates; AISD lowered its rate. For a $500,000 homestead, the five add up to about $8,215 a year. The City says a typical homeowner's City bill rises from about $2,074 to $2,190. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/government/where-austin-property-taxes-go-this-year/">Community Impact</a></span>

##### City dashboard lists canceled voter-approved bond projects
The City's new Austin Bond Dashboard shows the status of projects from four bond programs. KXAN counted 66 canceled, including sidewalks, intersections, bikeways and affordable housing developments; a City memo counts 61. Assistant City Manager Mike Rogers wrote that canceled projects' money has been or is being reallocated, and that staff will create a consistent process for deciding cancellations. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/city-of-austin-canceled-more-than-60-voter-approved-bond-projects-new-dashboard-shows/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Public safety & courts

##### One person dies after electrocution at downtown construction site
One person died Friday afternoon after being electrocuted at a construction site on Reserve Road downtown, the Austin Fire Department said. A second person was evaluated and not taken to a hospital. Firefighters said Austin Energy had to de-energize a nearby line and call a larger truck before crews could use a ladder truck bucket to reach both people. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/one-person-dead-at-construction-site-in-downtown-austin-apd-says/">KXAN</a></span>

##### Man charged with murder in bus stop killing
Austin police arrested Jesse C. Monroe II, 33, and charged him with murder in the death of Jeffrey Grable, 66, CBS Austin reports. A bus driver found Grable bleeding near a bus stop in the 1000 block of Norwood Park Boulevard early Tuesday. Police said Grable had been lying near the stop for hours when someone assaulted him and ran. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/suspect-arrested-charged-with-murder-in-deadly-assault-near-ne-austin-capmetro-bus-stop">CBS Austin</a></span>

A pedestrian was killed October 4 at Lake Creek Parkway and Pecan Park Boulevard; police said the driver was not impaired and is cooperating. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/apd-investigates-deadly-pedestrian-crash-in-northwest-austin">CBS Austin</a></span>

Police arrested three suspects in a string of September 13 armed robberies on East Sixth Street in which an Army service member was shot and seriously hurt. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/police-arrest-3-in-string-of-downtown-austin-robberies-that-injured-army-service-member">CBS Austin</a></span>

A driver charged with intoxication assault after an October 3 crash at East Riverside Drive and Ben White Boulevard is out on bond; his next hearing is October 19. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/woman-hospitalized-by-alleged-drunk-driver-suspect-was-out-on-bond/">KXAN</a></span>

Police Chief Lisa Davis told KXAN the City's investigation of a north Austin immigration agent shooting could finish in a couple of weeks; federal agencies have not shared requested evidence. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/crime/austin-police-expect-to-wrap-ice-shooting-investigation-up-in-a-couple-of-weeks/">KXAN</a></span>

City officials discussed at an October 1 committee meeting whether electric air taxis could help paramedics reach patients when traffic blocks ambulances; no program exists yet. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/could-air-taxis-help-austin-paramedics-beat-traffic-city-explores-evtol-program/">KXAN</a></span>

#### City Hall & county

##### Travis County clears delayed state voter records
Travis County has finished updating 32,000 voter registration records the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) sent shortly before the registration deadline, registrar Celia Israel said Friday. Staff worked nights and weekends. About 20,000 paper applications mailed by the deadline still need processing before early voting begins October 19, Israel said. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-10-09/austin-tx-travis-county-delayed-voter-registrations-dps">KUT</a></span>

##### Both sides make their case on the City audit measure
Proposition I would amend the City charter to require an independent audit of all City operations every five years. At a Wednesday rally, Council Member Vanessa Fuentes said consultants "must identify enough cuts to pay their own fees." Bill Aleshire, who drafted the amendment, said the consultant would be professional and "they aren't going to suggest silly cost savings." <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/10/08/austin-elections-audit-city-hall-proposition-i-save-austin-now/">Austin Current</a></span>

Council Member José Velásquez faces four challengers in District 3, where affordability and the Dog's Head development dominate the race, KUT reports. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-10-09/austin-city-council-district-3-election-2026">KUT</a></span>

Candidates in Austin's five City Council races had more than $817,000 available as of September, according to Community Impact's review of finance reports. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/election/check-out-austin-city-council-candidate-finances-as-election-day-nears/">Community Impact</a></span>

#### Schools

##### Burnet and Webb petition drive faces Thursday deadline
Parents seeking state closure of Burnet and Webb middle schools, as an alternative to a broader AISD takeover, need signatures from parents representing more than half of each school's students by October 15. As of Friday, parents of 11% of Burnet and 2% of Webb students had signed. The Austin Council of PTAs said it will help with outreach. A successful petition would not rule out other state interventions. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/10/09/austin-austinisd-burnet-webb-petition-state-takeover/">Austin Current</a></span>
