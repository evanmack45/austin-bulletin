## The Briefing

### A quarter of Travis County has voted. Five days left.

<!-- desk:kicker Election · Early voting -->
<!-- desk:figure 27% -->
<!-- desk:photo courthouse -->

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-26/early-ballots.png" alt="Bar chart from Travis County unofficial early voting totals through Oct. 25: 256,222 ballots cast and 948,575 registered voters.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County</figcaption>
</figure>

Through Sunday, Oct. 25, 256,222 ballots were cast: 242,040 in-person early votes, 13,449 mail ballots and 733 limited ballots. That's 27.01% of the county's 948,575 registered voters, by the county's unofficial daily totals. Sunday was the slowest day yet, at 18,612 in-person voters.

<p class="if-you">Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, Oct. 30. Any county site works. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. <a href="https://votetravis.gov/current-election-information/current-election/">Hours and locations</a>.</p>

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The county says some sites will have extended hours on the last two days.</p>

{% endbigstory %}
