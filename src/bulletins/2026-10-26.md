### More than a quarter of Travis County voters have now cast ballots, with five days of early voting left.

<!-- desk:kicker Election -->
<!-- desk:figure 27.01% -->

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-26/early-ballots.png" alt="Bar chart from Travis County unofficial early voting totals through Oct. 25: 256,222 ballots cast and 948,575 registered voters.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County</figcaption>
</figure>

Through Sunday, 256,222 people had voted, 27.01% of the county's 948,575 registered voters, according to the county's unofficial daily totals. That's 242,040 in-person early votes plus 13,449 mail ballots received. Sunday was the slowest day so far, with 18,612 in-person voters.

<p class="if-you"><strong>If you haven't voted:</strong> early voting runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, Oct. 30, at any Travis County polling site, and the county says some sites will have extended hours on the last two days. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. <a href="https://votetravis.gov/current-election-information/current-election/">Vote Travis current election page</a>.</p>

More than a quarter of Travis County voters have now cast ballots, with five days of early voting left. Early voting runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, Oct. 30, at any Travis County polling site, and the county says some sites will have extended hours on the last two days. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3.

Travis County will take comments next month on how it spent its federal community development money. The county's Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program is asking for public comment on its annual performance report for program year 2025. Comments open at 8 a.m. Nov. 4 and close at 5 p.m. Nov. 19, and there's a public hearing at 9 a.m. Nov. 17. The county says a draft of the report will be posted on its CDBG page starting Nov. 4, and the invitation is available in English and Spanish.

The City's festival of trees, pollinators and urban nature wraps up Sunday, Nov. 1, with free tree plantings, guided nature walks and pollinator programs around the city. The schedule is searchable by date and neighborhood at rootsandwingsfest.com.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Early voting runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, Oct. 30, and Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3.</p>
