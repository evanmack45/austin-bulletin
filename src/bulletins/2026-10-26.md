### More than a quarter of Travis County voters have now cast ballots, with five days of early voting left.

<!-- desk:kicker Election -->
<!-- desk:figure 27.01% -->

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-10-26/early-ballots.png" alt="Bar chart from Travis County unofficial early voting totals through Oct. 25: 256,222 ballots cast and 948,575 registered voters.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County</figcaption>
</figure>

Through Sunday, Oct. 25, 256,222 people had voted, 27.01% of the county's 948,575 registered voters, according to the county's unofficial daily totals: 242,040 in-person early votes plus 13,449 mail ballots received. Sunday was the slowest day so far, with 18,612 in-person voters.

<p class="if-you"><strong>If you haven't voted:</strong> early voting runs 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Friday, Oct. 30, at any Travis County polling site. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. <a href="https://votetravis.gov/current-election-information/current-election/">Check hours and locations</a>.</p>

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The county says some sites will have extended hours on the last two days.</p>

{% endbigstory %}

##### Travis County will take comments next month on how it spent its federal community development money.
<!-- desk:closes Nov 19 -->
The county's Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program is asking for public comment on its annual performance report for program year 2025. The county says a draft of the report will be posted on its CDBG page starting Nov. 4.
<p class="if-you"><span class="date-tag">Nov 19</span> <strong>If you want to comment:</strong> comments open at 8 a.m. Nov. 4 and close at 5 p.m. Nov. 19, and there's a public hearing at 9 a.m. Nov. 17. <a href="https://www.traviscountytx.gov/news/2026/2953-travis-county-cdbg-py25-annual-report">Read the county notice</a>.</p>
