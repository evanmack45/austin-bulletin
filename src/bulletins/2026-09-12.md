### One program's name keeps turning up in the county's budget

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-12/county-diversion-money.png" alt="Bar chart of new money added to the Travis County Fiscal Year 2027-28 budget at the September 10 markup: 3 million dollars reserved for outside indigent-defense counsel, 2 million dollars for the Supportive Housing Initiative Pipeline, 1.6 million dollars earmarked for felony case representation, 1.5 million dollars for public defender attorney pay, and 1 million dollars a year for the Travis County Transformation Project">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Travis County budget markup, via Community Impact</figcaption>
</figure>

The Central Health board approved a $650 million budget on Wednesday. Travis County commissioners advanced a $2.3
billion one on Thursday. The same households pay both. And both put their new money behind the same idea.

Central Health set aside $127.5 million for mental health services, $54 million of it new, and said those investments
align with No Wrong Door — the county program that treats mental health, addiction, homelessness and arrest as one
connected problem rather than five separate ones. Its rate sits 6% above the no-new-revenue rate. The district said
that could cost most county homesteads an extra $22 a year.

Commissioners worked the other end of the same pipeline. They doubled the reserve for outside defense lawyers to $3
million, added $1.5 million to public defender pay, and put $2 million into supportive housing. Law enforcement asked
for another $3.26 million for Axon Enterprise technology. Commissioners took no action on that request, and folded the
county's Axon contracts into one department instead.

The Downtown Austin Alliance named five priorities the same week, and No Wrong Door is one of them, with 17
organizations signed on. Its own downtown survey found 69% of respondents worried about public safety tied to
homelessness and behavioral health.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Travis County commissioners vote on the final budget Sept. 29.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Central Health raises the county tax rate to build out mental health care
The hospital district's board approved a $650 million budget on Wednesday that sets aside $127.5 million for mental
health services, $54 million of it new. Board chair Geronimo Rodriguez called the spending a down payment on a
connected system of care. The rate sits 6% above the no-new-revenue rate, which Central Health said could cost most
county homesteads an extra $22 a year. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/travis-county/central-health-board-approves-tax-increase-to-fund-mental-health-programs/">KXAN</a></span>

##### Commissioners move money toward defense lawyers and consolidate the Axon contracts
Travis County advanced an estimated $2.3 billion Fiscal Year 2027-28 budget at a Sept. 10 markup. The reserve for
outside indigent-defense counsel doubled to $3 million, attorney pay rose by $1.5 million, and supportive housing
gained $2 million in ongoing money. The county's newly centralized Axon Enterprise budget totals $6,832,000, including
$376,592 in existing Taser costs. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/city-county/travis-county-advances-budget-as-public-defenders-axon-compete-for-dollars/">Community Impact</a></span>

Law enforcement agencies asked for another $3.26 million for Axon technology. Commissioners took no action Thursday, voting instead to consolidate the county's Axon contracts under a single department. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/police/travis-county-texas-debate-axon-technology-budget-talks/269-87e4cb9f-f491-44d8-9185-7ffe187f5d92">KVUE</a></span>

Groups opposing the expansion urged commissioners to fund mental health, diversion and public defense instead. Commissioner Brigid Shea said body and vehicle camera footage is an essential part of accountability. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/police/travis-county-texas-debate-axon-technology-budget-talks/269-87e4cb9f-f491-44d8-9185-7ffe187f5d92">KVUE</a></span>
