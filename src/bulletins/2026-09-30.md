Travis County commissioners adopted a $2.3 billion budget on Tuesday. The line that matters most this week is the reserve
the county keeps for disasters: $35 million, up from $18 million. That account was empty. The county spent it down
responding to the July 2025 floods, which killed nine people and destroyed nearly 200 homes. On Tuesday evening the
National Weather Service posted a Flood Watch over Travis County and much of South Central Texas, running from 7 p.m. tonight
to 7 p.m. Friday, with 2 to 4 inches of rain forecast and isolated amounts up to 8.

Some homeowners will owe the county a little less. A home valued at $372,633 pays Travis County $1,439 this year, about $30
less than last, because values dipped slightly even as the budget rose 4%. The county is roughly a fifth of an Austin
property tax bill. County Judge Andy Brown said the reserve grew because the county has to plan for something larger than
one creek: "We could have flooding that affects a broader geographic area." Residents of Sandy Creek stood in that same
courtroom on Tuesday asking for sirens.

Austin is working its drains. Watershed Protection crews are clearing culverts at low-water crossings, and the city's own
list of the streets that flood first starts with Lamar Boulevard between Ninth and 15th.

#### Money & bills

##### The county budget trims a tax bill and refills a disaster fund
Travis County Commissioners Court adopted a $2.3 billion budget on Tuesday for the coming fiscal year. Spending rose 4%,
but property values dipped, so a home valued at $372,633 owes the county $1,439 — about $30 less than last year. The county
collects roughly a fifth of an Austin property tax bill and pays for the jail, the courts, roads, parks and
elections. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-29/travis-county-budget-mental-healthcare-natural-disasters-rail">KUT</a></span>

##### Texas diesel hit a record, and an Austin party bus company is paying it
The American Automobile Association (AAA) put Texas diesel at a record $5.98 a gallon earlier this month, nearly $3 above
the average a year ago. Peter Nicholas, who runs Austin Party Bus Rental, said a fill that once cost $80 to $100 ran him
$225 on Monday, and projects about $50,000 less revenue this year. "You either raise prices or you go out of
business," he said. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/sky-high-diesel-prices-crash-the-party-for-one-austin-business-owner/">KXAN</a></span>

The budget nearly doubles the county's reserve fund for disasters, to $35 million from $18 million. Travis County zeroed that account out responding to the July 2025 floods. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-09-29/travis-county-budget-mental-healthcare-natural-disasters-rail">KUT</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### The airport broke ground on a six-gate concourse to keep gates open during construction
Austin-Bergstrom International Airport started work Tuesday on Concourse M, about 37,000 square feet on the airport's west
side with four contact gates and two walk-out gates for narrow-body planes. It runs separately from Barbara Jordan
Terminal, reached by shuttle bus from Gate 13, and is meant to hold capacity while the larger expansion proceeds. The
airport is targeting 2028. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/austin-airport-breaks-ground-on-concourse-m-to-add-six-gates/">KXAN</a></span>

of them affordable, with 10,000 square feet of commercial space and 16 acres of impervious

#### City Hall & county

##### Paige Ellis needed 3,044 signatures for a third term and fell 62 short
Austin council members seeking a consecutive third term must petition 5% of their district's qualified voters onto the
ballot. Ellis turned in more than 3,000 names in mid-August and the city clerk struck enough to leave her short. She has
accepted the ruling but still questions whether valid signatures were
removed. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/28/austin-citycouncil-paigeellis-government-term-limits/">Austin Current</a></span>

Former Council Member Kathie Tovo, the only Austin member to clear the bar in the past decade, turned in 4,700 signatures against a 3,500 requirement in 2018 and won. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/28/austin-citycouncil-paigeellis-government-term-limits/">Austin Current</a></span>

Fred Lewis, who helped write the 10-1 district rules, said the petition threshold was meant to be hard: "You had to show that you had real support for a third term." <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/28/austin-citycouncil-paigeellis-government-term-limits/">Austin Current</a></span>

<p class="good-thing">On Sunday afternoon at the Long Center, a video played of Jonah Dillon rolling his wheelchair across a canvas with paint on the tires. That is how he makes his work. Dillon, who has muscular dystrophy, was one of six artists honored at Art Spark Texas' Artists of the Year celebration, now in its ninth year and landing on the organization's 30th anniversary. He got the Director's Commendation for inventiveness and adaptability, and said the award proves people respect what his art is saying. "I hope to show people that no matter what, if you want to do something, you can find a way to do it," he said. Another honoree, Donna Provencher, was recognized for directing a sensory-friendly production of "You're A Good Man, Charlie Brown" where the audience could get up and move around whenever they wanted.
