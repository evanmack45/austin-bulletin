### Election Day is Tuesday: where to vote and find results

Polls open at 7 a.m. and close at 7 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 3, and Travis County voters can vote at any county polling site. The county's Election Day site list is posted, and its election page has a wait-times section. Once polls close, the county posts results on its election results site, and its live turnout tracker for the Nov. 3 election is already up.

If you moved here from another Texas county and aren't registered here yet: the county says limited ballots were available only during early voting, at 5501 Airport Blvd.; call the county elections office at 512-238-8683 with eligibility questions.

<p class="if-you">You can check your registration and your personal ballot with the county's voter lookup. <a href="https://votetravis.gov/voter-lookup/">Voter lookup</a></p>

#### City Hall & county

##### About 500,900 registered voters haven't cast a ballot yet
<!-- desk:closes Nov 3 -->
Early voting brought in 447,689 ballots, 47.20% of the county's 948,575 registered voters. That leaves 500,886 registered voters who hadn't voted as of the end of early voting, a little more than half the rolls.
<p class="if-you"><span class="date-tag">Nov 3</span> Most early voters went in person: 427,412 in-person early votes made up 95.5% of the total, with 16,793 mail ballots (3.8%) and 3,484 limited ballots (0.8%). Mail ballots still arriving aren't counted in these numbers.</p>

##### City Hall: Central Health board picks Wednesday, full council Thursday
<!-- desk:week Nov 4–5 -->
The council's Public Health Committee meets at 10 a.m. Wednesday, Nov. 4, at City Hall, 301 W. Second St. It may act on appointments to the Central Health Board of Managers, after a closed-door session on the candidates, and on a city social services strategy, and it'll hear an update on the city's homeless encampment management work from David Gray, director of Austin Homeless Strategies and Operations.
<p class="if-you"><span class="date-tag">Nov 4</span> Public comment is allowed in person or by phone on action items. The full City Council meets at 10 a.m. Thursday, Nov. 5; as of Monday morning, Legistar lists 34 items for that meeting but no published agenda yet.</p>
