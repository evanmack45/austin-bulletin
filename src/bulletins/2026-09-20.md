### Regular is $3.94 in Austin. Last September it was $2.74.

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-20/austin-pump-price.png" alt="Bar chart of the average price of a gallon of regular unleaded gasoline in the Austin-San Marcos metropolitan area, as published by the American Automobile Association (AAA) on September 20, 2026. A year ago the average was 2 dollars and 74 cents. A month ago it was 3 dollars and 57 cents. A week ago it was 3 dollars and 89 cents. On September 20, 2026 it was 3 dollars and 94 cents.">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · AAA metro averages, gasprices.aaa.com</figcaption>
</figure>

The American Automobile Association (AAA) put the average gallon of regular unleaded in the Austin-San Marcos metro at
$3.94 on Sunday morning. A week ago the figure was $3.89, and a month ago $3.57.

Crude is the reason. AAA Texas spokesperson Daniel Armbruster said prices usually fall once the summer travel season
ends, and that oil hovering around $100 a barrel is pushing them the other way; he said Texas could finish with its most
expensive September average on record. Diesel already has one. AAA's Texas diesel average hit the highest figure the
group has ever recorded on Saturday, and diesel is what carries groceries to the shelf.

The one lever anyone has proposed is the state's 20-cent-a-gallon tax on gasoline and undyed diesel. Agriculture
Commissioner Sid Miller asked Gov. Greg Abbott to suspend it. Press Secretary Andrew Maheleris said no law or
constitutional provision lets a governor do that, and that Chapter 418 of the Texas Government Code authorizes a governor
to suspend regulations, not taxes.

#### Money & bills

##### Diesel in Austin is $5.97 a gallon, and Texas has set an all-time high
AAA's Texas diesel average reached the highest figure the group has on record on Saturday. In the Austin-San Marcos
metro a gallon of diesel was $5.97 on Sunday morning, against $5.73 a week earlier and $3.14 last September. Mid-grade
gasoline was $4.41 and premium $4.78. Texas last set a record for regular unleaded in June
2022. <span class="src"><a href="https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=TX">AAA</a></span>
