### Thursday is the registration deadline for a possible runoff.

<!-- desk:kicker Bulletin original -->

If you need to register to vote in a possible Dec. 12 city runoff, the deadline is Thursday, Nov. 12.

{% leadfigure %}

The city's runoff calendar schedules Council's canvass of the Nov. 3 municipal election for 11 a.m. Nov. 17 in Council Chambers. It says the actual canvass could come earlier once the counties deliver their returns. Council would order any necessary runoff on the day it canvasses. If Council orders a runoff, early voting by personal appearance would run Nov. 30 through Dec. 8, and Election Day would be Dec. 12. The calendar does not say a runoff has been ordered, and it does not name who would be on the ballot. Registration is the step a voter has to finish before that possible election, and the city calendar is the schedule for it.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> If you need to register to vote in a possible Dec. 12 city runoff, the deadline is Thursday, Nov. 12. Council's canvass is scheduled for 11 a.m. Nov. 17 but could occur earlier once counties provide returns; any necessary runoff would be ordered that same day. <a href="#runoff-calendar">Runoff calendar</a></p>

## News

{% river %}
##### Council draft proposes a boardwalk beside the Hyatt.
The Nov. 19 draft proposes a south-bank boardwalk beside the Hyatt Regency, and the item is a proposal, not an approval. It lists a total estimated project cost of $25 million, plus any cost overruns, separate from $25 million in state funds from the Texas Department of Transportation, and says a funding match is not required.
<p class="if-you">The agenda was still labeled a draft and unchanged since Friday afternoon. The segment would connect to the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail. Item 52 is related to item 11. The draft does not set a final cost or an overrun cap. The city says items can change before the final agenda, and Council will act only on the final agenda.</p>
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=484837">Nov. 19 draft agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2026/20261119-reg">Meeting page</a></span>

##### Draft contracts would rebuild Hornsby Bend treatment facilities.
The proposed construction would replace the plant's existing headworks and add a sludge receiving station, an ammonia removal facility and drainage improvements at Austin Water's Hornsby Bend biosolids plant. These are proposed contracts; Council will act only on the final agenda. Item 14's not-to-exceed cap of $22,420,200 and Item 15's separate not-to-exceed cap of $32,906,529 include contingencies and are not listed as money already spent.
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=484837">Nov. 19 draft agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2026/20261119-reg">Meeting page</a></span>

##### Draft amendments would change the city's Muny agreement with UT.
The Nov. 19 draft would let staff negotiate changes to the city–UT agreement for Muny and the surrounding area. Those changes cover development rules and expectations for transferring the course, and the item is proposed, not approved. Item 50 would amend the interlocal agreement with the University of Texas. The draft lists no fiscal impact for that item.
<p class="if-you">The proposal concerns the city–UT agreement setting development rules for the area around Muny and expectations for transferring the golf course, giving up city rights-of-way and creating a regulating plan and tax increment reinvestment zone. Council would be authorizing amendments to that agreement, not taking a final action on this draft agenda.</p>
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=484837">Nov. 19 draft agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2026/20261119-reg">Meeting page</a></span>

##### Water, parks, and transportation briefings are on this week's committee agendas.
The Climate, Water, Environment, and Parks Committee meets at 9:30 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 10, at City Hall for Austin Water and Pease Park Conservancy briefings. The Mobility Committee meets at 1 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 12, for the Core Transportation Plan, Parmer Lane, and mobility programs.
<p class="if-you">The water agenda covers the Balcones Canyonland Preserve, water quality in the Barton Springs segment of the Edwards Aquifer, and the city's partnership with the Pease Park Conservancy. Mobility will hear Urban Transportation Commission recommendations on Parmer Lane and a Transportation and Public Works report. Both committees may set 2027 meeting dates. Nov. 12 is a cancelled regular Council date, not a cancellation of the Mobility Committee. The next regular Council meeting is Nov. 19. A briefing is not a promise that the public can testify.</p>

##### Watson asked Friday for new Austin Police Department rules on immigration cooperation, KUT reports.
Mayor Kirk Watson said Friday he has asked Police Chief Lisa Davis to update the Austin Police Department's general orders on cooperating with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement so new rules can take effect when Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa takes office in January, KUT reports. The report is dated Nov. 6 and is not a new Sunday development.
<p class="if-you">KUT reports that the current rules say officers should contact ICE when operationally feasible when someone has an ICE administrative warrant, and that Watson said the city will keep following Senate Bill 4.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.kut.org/crime-justice/2026-11-06/austin-tx-apd-police-department-ice-policies-hinojosa-abbott">KUT</a></span>

##### Thompson said Friday she has the votes to be Texas House speaker.
State Rep. Senfronia Thompson, D-Houston, said Friday that a majority of the Texas House has pledged to back her for speaker, KUT and the Texas Newsroom reported Nov. 6. The speaker vote is Jan. 12 and takes 76 of 150 votes. Her statement is her claim of support, not a certified tally.
<p class="if-you">This is Friday's report, not a new Sunday development, and it is not a full list of speaker candidates.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-11-06/texas-house-speaker-senfronia-thompson-democrats">KUT / The Texas Newsroom</a></span>

##### A Nov. 6 police release seeks one suspect in copper-cable thefts.
The Austin Police Department's Nov. 6 release says thieves used vehicles to pull down wooden telephone poles in Northeast Austin starting in August to strip communication cable. The release says Adam Ray Lawrence, 37, was charged with theft of material (copper), a state jail felony, and booked in October. It says a warrant on the same charge is out for Clifford Nelson Locklin, 52.
<p class="if-you">The release does not say either man has been convicted. A charge is an accusation, not a finding of guilt. Tips go to the unit at (512) 974-6941 or Crime Stoppers at 512-472-8477. This is Friday's release, not a new Sunday development.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/apd-arrests-suspect-copper-cable-theft-series-seeks-public-assistance-locating-second">APD copper theft release</a></span>

##### Ten income-restricted townhomes were under construction on Menchaca Road as of Friday.
Austin Housing said Nov. 6 that construction has started on Menchaca Townhomes at 7331 Menchaca Road, 10 for-sale homes reserved for households earning at or below 80% of area median income. City guidelines cap prices at $253,000 for a two-bedroom and $289,500 for a three-bedroom. The release says the first three homes are planned for February 2027 and the rest for early 2028, and that details will come later.
<p class="if-you">The release does not say applications are open. This is Friday's announcement, not a new Sunday development.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/housing/news/new-affordable-menchaca-townhomes-bring-ten-homeownership-opportunities-south-austin">Austin Housing release</a></span>

##### Tentative Nov. 5 notes list a transit-lane commitment and a housing-policy update.
The city's Nov. 5 Action Notes say their information is tentative and do not capture vote details. They list item 22 as a consent approval committing up to $1 million for a center-running transit lane at Dean Keeton Street and Interstate 35. Item 26 is listed as an approval as amended directing the city manager to update the Strategic Housing Blueprint.
<p class="if-you">The transit lane would be incorporated into the Texas Department of Transportation's Interstate 35 Capital Express Central. The housing direction includes production and preservation goals, data, displacement, engagement, and consideration of a potential bond. The notes do not include roll-call tallies or the amendment text. The housing item is an instruction to update policy, not an approved bond. The transit item does not set an opening date.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/council_meetings/action_notes.cfm?mid=1520">Nov. 5 Action Notes</a></span>

Approved minutes for Council's Oct. 22 meeting are posted on the city's meeting page. The minutes say Council approved them Nov. 5. The Nov. 5 Action Notes, which the city labels tentative, also list that minutes vote as a consent approval and do not include a roll-call tally.
