## The Briefing

### Last day to register for a possible Dec. 12 runoff.

<!-- desk:kicker Bulletin original -->

Thursday, Nov. 12, is the last day to register to vote in a Dec. 12 runoff.

That deadline falls before Council is scheduled to canvass the Nov. 3 results at 11 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 17, in Council Chambers and to order any necessary runoff the same day. The calendar says the actual canvass could come earlier once the counties deliver their returns. If Council orders a runoff, early voting by personal appearance would run Nov. 30 through Dec. 8, and Election Day would be Dec. 12.

The same week, the Climate, Water, Environment, and Parks Committee meets at 9:30 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 10, at City Hall for briefings on Austin Water's management of the Balcones Canyonland Preserve and water quality in the Barton Springs segment of the Edwards Aquifer, and on the city's partnership with the Pease Park Conservancy; the committee may also set its 2027 meeting dates. The Mobility Committee meets at 1 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 12, at City Hall for a briefing on near-term work under the Austin Core Transportation Plan, Urban Transportation Commission recommendations on the Parmer Lane project, and a Transportation and Public Works report on mobility programs. The regular Council meeting is Thursday, Nov. 19; a draft agenda is posted and is not final. Nov. 12 is a cancelled regular Council date on the 2026 meeting schedule.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Council is scheduled to canvass Nov. 3 results at 11 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 17, and to order any necessary runoff the same day. The calendar says that canvass could come earlier.</p>

##### Council's Nov. 19 draft agenda includes a $25 million Lady Bird Lake boardwalk.
The draft agenda for the Nov. 19 Council meeting, still labeled a draft and unchanged since Friday afternoon, would accept $25 million from the Texas Department of Transportation, with no city match required, for a Lady Bird Lake boardwalk, plus any cost overruns (item 52, related to item 11). The two Matous Construction contracts at Hornsby Bend have not-to-exceed caps of $22,420,200 and $32,906,529 (items 14 and 15).
<p class="if-you">The boardwalk would be built on the south bank next to the Hyatt Regency, connecting to the Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail. The contracts are for Austin Water's Hornsby Bend biosolids plant: new headworks and a sludge receiving station, and an ammonia removal facility and drainage work. Item 50 would amend the city's agreement with the University of Texas on land-development rules around Lions Municipal Golf Course (Muny), its planned conveyance, right-of-way vacations, a regulating plan and a tax increment reinvestment zone, with no fiscal impact listed. The city says items can change, be removed or be added before the final agenda, and Council will act only on the final agenda.</p>
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=484837">Nov. 19 draft agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2026/20261119-reg">Meeting page</a></span>

##### Austin will rewrite the Austin Police Department's (APD) rules on working with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) before Hinojosa takes office, KUT reports.
Mayor Kirk Watson said Friday he has asked Police Chief Lisa Davis to update APD's general orders on cooperating with Immigration and Customs Enforcement so new rules can take effect when Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa replaces Gov. Greg Abbott in January, KUT reports. APD has changed the policy twice in the past year: once in March to limit ICE involvement in local policing, and again after Abbott threatened to withhold state money.
<p class="if-you">The current rules say officers should contact ICE "when operationally feasible" when someone has an ICE administrative warrant. Watson said the city will keep following Senate Bill 4, the state law barring cities from blocking cooperation with ICE.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.kut.org/crime-justice/2026-11-06/austin-tx-apd-police-department-ice-policies-hinojosa-abbott">KUT</a></span>

##### Police charge two in Northeast Austin copper-cable thefts and seek one of them.
APD's Commercial Burglary and Metal Theft Unit says thieves used vehicles to pull down wooden telephone poles in Northeast Austin starting in August to strip AT&T communication cable. Police say Adam Ray Lawrence, 37, sold more than 600 pounds of copper days after one theft; he was charged with theft of material (copper), a state jail felony, and booked into the Travis County Jail in October.
<p class="if-you">A warrant on the same charge is out for Clifford Nelson Locklin, 52, who police believe lives in Bastrop. Tips go to the unit at (512) 974-6941 or Crime Stoppers at 512-472-8477, with a reward of up to $1,000. A charge is an accusation, not a finding of guilt.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/police/news/apd-arrests-suspect-copper-cable-theft-series-seeks-public-assistance-locating-second">APD copper theft release</a></span>

##### Senfronia Thompson says she has the votes to be Texas House speaker.
State Rep. Senfronia Thompson, D-Houston, said Friday that a majority of the Texas House has pledged to back her for speaker, which would make her the first woman and first Black lawmaker to lead the chamber, the Texas Newsroom reports via KUT. Austin Democratic Reps. Lulu Flores and Aicha Davis stood with her at the Capitol announcement. The vote comes when the Legislature convenes Jan. 12; it takes 76 of 150 votes.
<p class="if-you">Thompson has served since 1973.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.kut.org/politics/2026-11-06/texas-house-speaker-senfronia-thompson-democrats">KUT / The Texas Newsroom</a></span>

##### Ten affordable townhomes for sale are under construction on Menchaca Road.
Construction has started on Menchaca Townhomes at 7331 Menchaca Road in District 5, 10 for-sale homes reserved for households earning at or below 80% of area median income, Austin Housing announced Friday. City affordable-housing guidelines cap prices at $253,000 for a two-bedroom and $289,500 for a three-bedroom.
<p class="if-you">The project by Capital A Housing received $1.9 million from the Austin Housing Finance Corporation's ownership program, funded by the Housing Trust Fund and voter-approved bonds. Work began in early August; the first three homes are due in February 2027 and the rest by early 2028. Buyers will go through income verification with Austin Housing, and the city says details will come later.</p>
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/housing/news/new-affordable-menchaca-townhomes-bring-ten-homeownership-opportunities-south-austin">Austin Housing release</a></span>
