### The mayor's plan puts the takeover fight in parents' hands

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-10/aisd-petition-clock.png" alt="Timeline of the deadlines in the state takeover question facing the Austin school district: Webb and Burnet middle schools received a fifth straight preliminary failing rating in August 2026, the district must appeal those preliminary ratings by September 11, parents must present a closure petition to the superintendent for validation by October 15, and the superintendent must forward a validated petition to the education commissioner by December 1">
<figcaption>Timeline: The Austin Bulletin · Austin Current and KUT</figcaption>
</figure>

Mayor Kirk Watson asked parents at Burnet and Webb middle schools this week to petition the state to close their own
campuses. The ask is strange. Both schools drew a fifth straight failing rating from the Texas Education Agency in
August, which under state law obliges Commissioner Mike Morath to act. He can close the campuses, or he can replace the
elected board of the Austin Independent School District (Austin ISD) with appointees. Watson's argument is that a parent
petition takes the second option off the table. The district has until Friday to appeal the ratings.

Two other things are true about the district those parents are being asked to save. Its buildings keep failing in the
heat — Kealing Middle School has been fighting a broken chiller since last Thursday, the fourth campus with air
conditioning trouble since Aug. 27. And it is $205 million into a round of cuts that took $1 million from Communities in
Schools. Nonprofits are now raising what the budget no longer covers.

Parents at Webb answered the mayor in public. "Can the mayor make that statement in Spanish?" the campus
[parent-teacher association wrote](#schools).

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Austin ISD's deadline to appeal the two preliminary ratings is tomorrow, Sept. 11.</p>

#### Public safety & courts

##### Two children were taken from a Ben White crash in critical condition

crash called in about 7:40 a.m. Wednesday. Austin-Travis County Emergency Medical Services took two children to

helicopter also responded. The intersection with westbound Ben White closed as well. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/traffic-alert-serious-crash-shuts-down-i-35-at-woodward/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### Lake Austin drops 10 feet next month, and the boat businesses found out in August
The Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) will lower Lake Austin about a foot a week from Oct. 12 until it sits 10 feet
down, refilling by Nov. 30. The city asked for the drawdown, and shoreline owners get exposed lakebed for repairs and
dredging. Owners of boat rentals and lakeside restaurants say seven weeks was not enough warning for a fall that carries
Austin City Limits, Formula 1 and football. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/curveball-lake-austin-to-lower-10-feet-this-fall-businesses-say-the-notice-came-too-late/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Schools

##### Watson asks Burnet and Webb parents to petition to close their schools
The mayor's proposal, released Wednesday, would have parents of a majority of students at either campus ask
Commissioner Mike Morath to close it, which state law generally requires him to grant. Watson wants the district to
then open a new school that keeps each community together. Parents must file by Oct. 15, and the superintendent
