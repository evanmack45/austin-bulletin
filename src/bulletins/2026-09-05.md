## The Briefing

### The budget passed last month. The bills arrived this week.

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-05/aisd-deficit.png" alt="Bar chart of the Austin Independent School District budget deficit for the 2025-26 school year at three points: 19 million dollars when the board adopted the budget, 121 million dollars after a mid-year amendment, and 105 million dollars in the final unaudited figure reported to the board on Aug. 27">
<figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin · Austin Independent School District data, via Community Impact</figcaption>
</figure>

Austin City Council adopted a $6.6 billion budget in August. This week four institutions showed what that arithmetic
does downstream, and none of them answered the same way.

The Austin Independent School District (Austin ISD) told its board Aug. 27 that last year's deficit closed at $105
million, against $19 million at adoption. Revenue came in $132 million short, mostly from property value protests and
a delayed land sale. Its state financial rating dropped to a B, the first time since at least 2017. The district
absorbs it.

Farmshare Austin has no such cushion. The nonprofit says city cuts take about $510,000 out of its Fresh for Less
produce program over two years, starting in November, and it is asking the public to cover the gap. The city had been
paying roughly 64 percent of that program.

Austin-Bergstrom International Airport went looking for someone else to charge. As early as Oct. 1, private and other
noncommercial aircraft face a $50 landing fee, and $100 on Formula 1, South by Southwest and University of Texas home
game weekends. Council Member Vanessa Fuentes said the aim is to make "private wealth work for public good."
Recreational pilots and flight students pay the same fee.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> Central Health's board adopts its fiscal 2027 tax rate and budget Wednesday, Sept. 9.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Central Health puts a price list behind its tax question
The hospital district's board weighed three paths at a Wednesday hearing: a 6 or 8 percent property tax rate
increase, or a no-new-revenue rate that holds bills flat. A raise would add $22.1 million for mental health, which the
district would pair with $18.5 million from a Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) settlement. President and
chief executive Dr. Patrick Lee said every additional dollar goes to mental health. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/central-health-considers-raising-travis-county-taxes-for-mental-health-resources/">KXAN</a></span>

##### The school district ends the year $86 million past its own budget
Superintendent Matias Segura and chief financial officer Katrina Montgomery told trustees Aug. 27 that the 2025-26
shortfall finished $86 million above what the board adopted, at $105 million, and the fund balance fell to 8.8
percent against a 15.21 percent target. Required spending rose a net $41 million on teacher retirement costs and new
state funding rules. Montgomery said one pass-fail staffing measure cost the rating. <span class="src"><a href="https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/education/aisds-financial-rating-slips-as-deficit-lands-at-105m/">Community Impact</a></span>

Austin plans to charge noncommercial aircraft a $50 landing fee at the airport as early as Oct. 1, raise hangar rents 3 percent and lift the fuel fee to 12 cents. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/04/austin-airport-private-jet-plane-fee/">Austin Current</a></span>

Farmshare Austin says city cuts starting in November take about $510,000 from a produce program it says reaches roughly 33,000 Austinites; it is fundraising to keep its staff. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kvue.com/article/news/community/farmshare-austin-texas-seeks-community-support-losing-city-funding/269-c8dc62b9-b381-496a-a8a3-46ea35810f95">KVUE</a></span>

Diesel in the Austin area hit a record $5.51 a gallon Friday and regular averaged $3.74, with the American Automobile Association's Doug Johnson saying the Iran conflict has made prices hard to predict. <span class="src"><a href="https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-diesel-hits-record-551-as-aus-enters-peak-labor-day-travel-day">CBS Austin</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### CapMetro's bus contractor and its drivers settle a three-year contract
Amalgamated Transit Union Local 1091 and Keolis, the subcontractor that employs CapMetro's bus operators and
maintenance workers, reached a collective bargaining agreement covering 2026 through 2028. It raises wages 3 percent
this year, 4 percent next year and 5 percent in 2028, pays a $1,500 ratification bonus and adds time-and-a-half
during declared emergencies. It still needs to be ratified. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/capmetro-3-year-agreement-reached-between-union-subcontractor/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Schools

##### The state board signs off on the new social studies standards
The State Board of Education gave final approval Friday to revised high school standards in U.S. history, government
and world geography, including language saying radical Islam motivated the Sept. 11 attacks. Muslim speakers had
objected that the early Islam section dwells on military campaigns, slavery and female captives. Board member Julie
Pickren, who worked on the underlying law, called the vote "such a huge accomplishment." <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/texas-education-board-approves-controversial-school-curriculum-changes">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

Texas approved Alpha School, the Austin-founded network whose artificial intelligence software teaches lessons in two hours a day, for its voucher program after the state board rejected its charter bid. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/texas/texas-rejected-an-ai-driven-schools-bid-to-open-a-charter-then-the-state-approved-it-for-the-voucher-program/">KXAN</a></span>
