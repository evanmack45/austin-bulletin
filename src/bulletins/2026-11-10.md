## The Briefing

### Rent limits end in 2027 for 927 Austin affordable rentals.

<!-- desk:kicker Bulletin original -->

Austin's Affordable Housing Inventory lists 927 rent-restricted rental units with affordability expiration dates in 2027, more than three times the 268 listed for 2028.

{% leadfigure %}

Those 927 units (918 apartments, plus 9 in duplexes and single-family homes) sit on 11 completed rental projects in the city's inventory. Four apartment communities account for 882 of them: Cascades at Onion Creek in District 5 (264 affordable units, listed expiration July 15, 2027), Runnymede Apartments in District 4 (252 units, Oct. 1), Legacy Ranch @ Dessau East in District 7 (186 of 232 total units, April 6), and Meadowood Apartments in District 4 (180 of 200 total units, May 1). Districts 4, 5 and 7 together account for 883 of the 927 units.

The inventory records the date a project's listed affordability period expires. It does not say whether rents will rise, whether a lease will change, or whether a restriction will be extended or replaced. The 927-unit count includes only rental properties marked "Project Completed" with expiration dates in calendar year 2027. Rows with placeholder dates, such as 3799 or 1939, were excluded. The dataset can be revised; these figures were pulled the evening of Nov. 9, 2026, in America/Chicago (CT), and matched a second live query minutes later.

## News

{% river %}
##### County court weighs $197 million in borrowing for 2027 projects.
Travis County commissioners' 9 a.m. agenda today asks them to raise the proposed fiscal 2027 certificates of obligation issuance by $13,475,000, to a total of $197,125,000. It's tax-exempt debt for approved capital projects, and the same item would front $99,302,059 from the county's Unallocated Reserve until the debt proceeds arrive. The agenda lists items for consideration; it does not say any are approved.
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://countyclerk.traviscountytx.gov/wp-content/uploads/commissioners_court/11-10-2026VSAgenda.pdf">Nov. 10 agenda (PDF)</a></span>

##### $67.8 million contract for new STAR Flight helicopters is up today.
A late item on today's county agenda would award the purchase of STAR Flight helicopters to the highest-ranked bidder, Bell Textron Inc., for $67,757,745.12. STAR Flight is the county's 24/7 air ambulance and rescue service, which the county says now flies three Leonardo AW169 helicopters.
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://countyclerk.traviscountytx.gov/wp-content/uploads/commissioners_court/11-10-2026VSAgenda.pdf">Nov. 10 agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.traviscountytx.gov/star-flight">Travis County STAR Flight</a></span>

##### County could start work on a 2027 bond election today.
Commissioners will consider approving preparation of a 2027 bond referendum and creating a 2027 Citizens Bond Advisory Committee. A bond referendum would put county borrowing to voters; the agenda doesn't yet list projects or a dollar amount. Item 31 is on the Nov. 10 agenda.
<span class="src">Source: <a href="https://countyclerk.traviscountytx.gov/wp-content/uploads/commissioners_court/11-10-2026VSAgenda.pdf">Nov. 10 agenda (PDF)</a></span>

##### Ann Kitchen is up for another Central Health board term.
Today's county agenda lists the reappointment of Ann Kitchen to the Central Health Board of Managers for a term ending Dec. 31, 2030. Central Health is Travis County's hospital district; the county and the city each appoint four of its board managers, plus one jointly.
<span class="src">Sources: <a href="https://countyclerk.traviscountytx.gov/wp-content/uploads/commissioners_court/11-10-2026VSAgenda.pdf">Nov. 10 agenda (PDF)</a> · <a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/boards-commissions/board/central-health-board-managers">Central Health Board of Managers</a></span>
