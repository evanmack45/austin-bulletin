### Water costs more in six days, and the drought map just went extreme

<figure class="graphic">
<img src="/images/2026-09-25/water-rates-tiers.png" alt="Bar chart comparing Austin Water's current residential volume charge per 1,000 gallons with the charge that takes effect October 1, 2026, for the first three monthly usage tiers, from the utility's rate schedule filed with the Austin City Council. <figcaption>Chart: The Austin Bulletin &middot; City of Austin, Austin Water rate schedule</figcaption>

Austin Water's rate schedule for the budget year that starts Oct. 1 raises the residential volume charge at every
tier. A household using between 6,001 and 11,000 gallons a month goes from $10.26 per 1,000 gallons to $11.24.
Single-family wastewater above 2,000 gallons goes from $13.40 to $14.93.

The same week, the U.S. Drought Monitor put parts of Travis County in extreme drought for the first time since June 2.
A stubborn high-pressure ridge has kept the rain off and the heat on.

The city also called off its plan for Lake Austin. The 10-foot drawdown set for Oct. 12 through Nov. 30 is dead, after
lakefront businesses said the timing would cost jobs in wedding season and during University of Texas at Austin
football and Formula 1. The Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) offered Dec. 1 through Jan. 15 instead, which ran
into holiday events, and it has not lowered the lakes in winter since Winter Storm Uri, because the Highland Lakes dams
hold power in reserve for the grid. The hydrilla stays. Permit refunds are going out.

What the city's biggest new water customers use is still not on paper anywhere. The Texas attorney general's office is
investigating data centers in Travis and 17 other counties that never answered the Texas Water Development Board's
survey asking how much water they draw.

<p class="whats-next"><strong>What's next:</strong> The new water and wastewater rates take effect Oct. 1. The city said staff will keep weighing a future drawdown with LCRA; today's numbers and the week ahead are in <a href="#weather">Weather</a>, and the rest of the city's money is in <a href="#money-bills">Money &amp; bills</a>.</p>

#### Money & bills

##### Austin Water's rates go up at every tier on Oct. 1
The utility's published schedule for the coming budget year lifts the residential volume charge from $3.27 to $3.57 per
1,000 gallons on the first 2,000 gallons, and from $20.40 to $22.21 at the top tier. The five fixed minimum charges
rise at all five tiers too, the smallest from $1.36 to $1.40 and the largest from $35.54 to $37. Customer Assistance
Program households keep a waiver on that minimum. <span class="src"><a href="https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=478626">City of Austin</a></span>

##### Austin schools will borrow $140 million in October to make payroll
Chief Financial Officer Katrina Montgomery told trustees Thursday the district must find $5.7 million more in cuts this
year to avoid a deficit, and that low reserves mean borrowing through November at an estimated $2.1 million in interest
and fees. "There is no savings account that we can go into," she said. The loan is repaid in January, once property tax
money lands. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/24/austin-austinisd-enrollment-transportation-budget/">Austin Current</a></span>

#### Growth & infrastructure

##### The Lake Austin drawdown is off, and there is no new date
A joint Austin Water and Watershed Protection memo to council said the city heard from businesses about the fall
wedding season, University of Texas football and Formula 1, and that the winter window LCRA offered would have hit
holiday events on the lake. Staff will keep evaluating. People who bought permits for shoreline work are getting
refunds. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/lake-austin-2026-drawdown-canceled">City of Austin</a></span>

##### Council cleared the way for up to 1,000 apartments in Circle C, next to an elementary school
Two Thursday items rezoned Tract 110 from office to multifamily for developer Stratus and dropped a requirement to
build the South Bay Lane extension. The Protect Circle C Coalition's Lisa Lyons said the replacement access point on
Dahlgreen Avenue sits about 500 feet from Kiker Elementary and sends traffic through the school
zone. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.fox7austin.com/news/circle-c-traffic-impacts-proposed-apartments">FOX 7 Austin</a></span>

The Texas attorney general's office is investigating data center developments in Travis and 17 other counties that did not answer the Texas Water Development Board's survey on their water use. <span class="src"><a href="https://www.kxan.com/news/texas/hundreds-of-texas-data-centers-under-investigation-after-failing-to-report-water-usage/">KXAN</a></span>

#### Schools

##### Austin ISD is short more than 2,000 students, and the money follows the child
About 6,400 students who finished last year did not come back, while 8,500 new ones arrived, district leaders said
Thursday. Of those who left, 41% went somewhere the district cannot identify, 39% moved away and 20% went to charter,
private, virtual or home schooling. The gap could cost an estimated $14 million in
revenue. <span class="src"><a href="https://austincurrent.org/2026/09/24/austin-austinisd-enrollment-transportation-budget/">Austin Current</a></span>
