Chart for the November 5, 2026 edition lead. This is an original chart
by The Austin Bulletin, not a photograph and not an AI image.

The figures are monthly parking enforcement requests to Austin 311,
June 2025 through October 2026, from the City of Austin Open Data
dataset "311 Service Requests" (xwdj-i9he). October 2026 is 2,437.
Source: https://data.austintexas.gov/d/xwdj-i9he

parking-311.svg is the source drawing.
parking-311.webp is 1280 by 960.
parking-311-800.webp is 800 by 600.
Both WebP files are rasterized from the SVG and scaled only, not cropped.
Each file is under 120KB.

The y-axis reads "Parking enforcement requests to 311".
Every month label names that month in plain words and gives its count.
