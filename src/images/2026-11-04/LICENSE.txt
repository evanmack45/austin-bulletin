Chart for the November 4, 2026 edition lead. This is an original chart
by The Austin Bulletin, not a photograph and not an AI image.

The figures are the ones already printed in that edition, from Travis
County's unofficial results for the November 3, 2026 election:
Proposition A (parks) 61.4 percent for, Proposition B (libraries) 60.3
percent for, Proposition I (labeled Prop I) 56.0 percent against, turnout 58.7 percent of
registered voters, and a December 12 runoff for three council races.
Source: https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Travis/127492/
