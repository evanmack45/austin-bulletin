## Austin City Council races 2026: Districts 1, 3, 5, 8 and 9

**Last checked: October 14, 2026.** Five of Austin's ten Council districts are on the November 3, 2026 ballot. Two seats are open. Three incumbents are seeking re-election. This page lists every candidate in ballot order and links each incumbent's voting record. It does not say how to vote. The Austin Bulletin does not endorse.

Not sure which district you live in? Look up your address on [Y'all, City Hall: Your District](https://yallcityhall.org/district/). Only voters in Districts 1, 3, 5, 8 and 9 see a Council race on their ballot this year. The mayor is not on the ballot.

Early voting runs **October 19–30**, and Election Day is **November 3**. Sites, hours and the rest of the ballot are on our [Vote 2026 guide](/vote-2026/).

### District 3

- Eduardo "Lalito" Romero
- Brock Skinner
- José Velásquez (incumbent)
- Gavin Fernandez Jr.
- Neha Shah

**José Velásquez's record:** 97% attendance from January 2023 to August 27, 2026, the latest official roll call. He voted **no** on the August 4 resolution that started the parks and library bond election, which passed 8-3, and yes on the August 6 ordinances that put the bonds and the charter amendments on the ballot. Full record: [Y'all, City Hall: José Velásquez](https://yallcityhall.org/scorecards/jose-velasquez/) · [August 4, item 2](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-04/#item-2)

**Ryan Alter's record:** 97% attendance from January 2023 to August 27, 2026. He voted **yes** on the August 4 bond resolution and on the August 6 bond and charter election ordinances. Full record: [Y'all, City Hall: Ryan Alter](https://yallcityhall.org/scorecards/ryan-alter/) · [August 4, item 2](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-04/#item-2)

### District 8 (open seat)

- Jeffery Bowen
- Selena Xie

Selena Xie led the Austin EMS Association. Jeffery Bowen previously ran for mayor. [KUT, August 25](https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-08-25/austin-tx-city-council-paige-ellis-third-term-petition-rejected)

Paige Ellis has served two four-year terms, the Charter's limit unless a member files a petition signed by 5% of the district's qualified voters. She needed 3,044 signatures and submitted more than 3,400. The City found the petition 62 valid signatures short and rejected it. Her term ends in January. [KUT, August 25](https://www.kut.org/austin/2026-08-25/austin-tx-city-council-paige-ellis-third-term-petition-rejected) · Her record: [Y'all, City Hall: Paige Ellis](https://yallcityhall.org/scorecards/paige-ellis/)

### District 9

- Rich Heyman
- Katie Kam
- Zohaib "Zo" Qadri (incumbent)
- Dave Thadani
- Kai Huang

**Zo Qadri's record:** 98% attendance from January 2023 to August 27, 2026. He voted **yes** on the August 4 bond resolution and on the August 6 bond and charter election ordinances. On the August 12 budget, which passed 9-2 with Mayor Kirk Watson and Council Member Marc Duchen voting no, he said: "This budget isn't perfect, but I'm proud that we were able to protect some of the services Austinites depend on most." Full record: [Y'all, City Hall: Zo Qadri](https://yallcityhall.org/scorecards/zohaib-zo-qadri/) · [August 4, item 2](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-04/#item-2) · [Austin Current, August 12](https://austincurrent.org/2026/08/12/austin-city-budget-taxes-texas/)

### The 2026 votes all three incumbents cast

- **August 4, parks and library bond resolution, 8-3:** Velásquez no; Alter and Qadri yes. Watson and Duchen also voted no. [Y'all, City Hall: August 4, item 2](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-04/#item-2)
- **August 6, bond election ordered** and **charter amendment election ordered:** all three yes. [Item 2](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-06/#item-2) · [Item 4](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-06/#item-4)
- **August 12, the 2026-27 budget, 9-2:** all three were among the nine yes votes; Watson and Duchen voted no. [Austin Current, August 12](https://austincurrent.org/2026/08/12/austin-city-budget-taxes-texas/) · [Y'all, City Hall: August 12, item 7](https://yallcityhall.org/meetings/2026-08-12/#item-7)

What the bonds and the charter amendments would do: [Propositions A and B](/austin-bonds-2026/) · [Propositions C–J](/austin-charter-2026/). What the budget did: [Austin's 2026-27 city budget](/austin-city-budget-2027/).

### If no one wins a majority

The City has scheduled a runoff candidate panel, if one is needed, for **November 20, 6:30 p.m.**, in City Hall Council Chambers, 301 W. 2nd St. [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/communications/news/public-invited-attend-2026-austin-city-council-candidate-panels) We will add the runoff date when the County posts it.
