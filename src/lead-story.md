# Rent limits end in 2027 for 927 Austin affordable rentals

Austin's Affordable Housing Inventory lists 927 rent-restricted rental units with affordability expiration dates in 2027, more than three times the 268 listed for 2028.

Those 927 units (918 apartments, plus 9 in duplexes and single-family homes) sit on 11 completed rental projects in the city's inventory. Four apartment communities account for 882 of them: Cascades at Onion Creek in District 5 (264 affordable units, listed expiration July 15, 2027), Runnymede Apartments in District 4 (252 units, Oct. 1), Legacy Ranch @ Dessau East in District 7 (186 of 232 total units, April 6), and Meadowood Apartments in District 4 (180 of 200 total units, May 1). Districts 4, 5 and 7 together account for 883 of the 927 units.

The inventory records the date a project's listed affordability period expires. It does not say whether rents will rise, whether a lease will change, or whether a restriction will be extended or replaced. The 927-unit count includes only rental properties marked "Project Completed" with expiration dates in calendar year 2027. Rows with placeholder dates, such as 3799 or 1939, were excluded. The dataset can be revised; these figures were pulled the evening of Nov. 9, 2026, in America/Chicago (CT), and matched a second live query minutes later.

Source: [City of Austin Affordable Housing Inventory](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/ifzc-3xz8).
