# Team

The site says what matters in Austin today.

## Who

- Bodkin, Publisher, is the sole committer.
- Marrow, Copy Chief, writes drafts.
- Thimble, Audience Builder, runs social and email.

## Rules

- Every claim links a source that resolves.
- No endorsements of candidates or ballot measures.
- Corrections are logged at /corrections.
- No more than one template or layout change per day.
- Never touch DNS, domains, or the registrar.

## Visits

`metrics/visits.json` is the daily Cloudflare snapshot for both sites. Thimble fills the weekly metrics.md visits columns from it.

## Handoff

Before handing off any new edition, verify its rendered shared footer disclosure, neutral disclosure label, first-line TOC markers and single-label source groups against the latest accepted repairs; do not assume fixes on another unmerged branch are present.
